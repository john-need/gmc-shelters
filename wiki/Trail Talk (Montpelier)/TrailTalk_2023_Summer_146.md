     AFSP was lush with spring growth – trees leaf-
ing out and dozens of plants budding and flowering.
To my dismay, blood root had already flowered and
gone to seed! (I have since seen it in flower further
north). There were however many varieties of tril-
lium, Solomon’s seal, Solomon’s plume, early blue
cohosh, Mayapple, violets, and more.
     That first afternoon, I hiked 7.3 miles to Black
Gap Shelter, where I camped. I met others starting on
the adventure of a lifetime. I kept my miles low the
first few days. When I increased my distances I left
behind some new friends and made others.
     As of April 25th, I had hiked over 125 trail miles
plus the 8.5 mile Approach Trail. My body feels good
except for some blisters.
     Other than one particularly warm day, the tem-
peratures have been much cooler than I expected:
usually 50’s during the day and often dipping to the
30’s at night. It is not easy getting out of the tent in
the morning when it is so cold.
