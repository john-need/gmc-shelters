MONTPELIER SECTION - GREEN MOUNTAIN CLUB
                                        OUTING SCHEDULE

                                   October 1979 - February 1980

      This schedule is prepared by the Montpelier Section of the Green Mountain Club , Inc.,
which has its headquarters in Montpelier, Vermont. Membership in the section automatically
results in membership in the main club and in a subscription to THE LONG TRAIN NEWS which
is published quarterly. A portion of each section member's dues is forwarded to the main
club.
     While our reason for being is to maintain a section of the Long Trail north of Smug-
glers Notch, we also schedule a full year of varied outdoor activities for the pleasure of
our members. Guests are always welcome to participate. Outings are planned to vary in
difficulty to suit all abilities. When in doubt, call the leader. Attendance at the sche-
duled planning meetings is encouraged as this is where ideas for outings are generated .
Except for meals, advance registr ation is not required, but is always useful to the leader
and to inform you of any last minute changes . Unless otherwise stated, all trips will meet
at the Montpelier High School parking lot . Officers and dues are listed at the end of this
schedule. COME OUT WITH US - MARK YOUR CALENDAR WITH THESE DATES:
