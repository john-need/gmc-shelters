I                                 SUMMER SCHEDULE - 1965

    MONTPELIER SECTION                                          GREEN MOUNTAIN CLUB


    Sunday, May 23       - A Sunday afternoon walk in Middlesex, near Brock's Camp
                           Road, about four miles. Leave Montpelier at 1:30 p.m.

                                                       Leader - Julia Desilets (3-7525)
                                         *'.,. * * * * * *
    Saturday, June 5     - Trail clearing time!! Bring axes, saws, etc., and lunch.
         and               Leave Montpelier at 8:30 a.m. both days.
    Sunday, June 6
                                           Saturday Leader - Dave Otis (3-3190)
                                             Sunday Leader - Dave Morse (3-7126)
                                          *******
    Sunday, June 20      - Leave Montpelier at 8:00 a.m. Start climbing at Couching
                           Lion Farm, hike to Montclair Glen Lodge, over Ethan and
                           Ira Allen Mountains, Burnt Rock Mountain and down the
                           Hedgehog Brook Trail. Approximately nine miles. Bring
                           big trail lunch.

                                                       Leader - Dick Babcock (3-7498)
                                          ********
    Sunday, June 27      - Sunday afternoon walk on back road off the Elmore Road in
                           the Worcester area. About four miles. Leave Montpelier
                           at 1:30 p.m.

                                                       Leader - Dave Hine (3-6778)
                                          *********
    Sunday, July 11      - Family supper cookout at Underhill State Camping Area.
                           Fires available. Bring own food (hotdogs, hamburgers,
                           etc.). Coffee and cocoa will be served.

                           Short Hike:    to Cantilever Rock, about 3.5 miles

                                                       Leader - Dave Otis (3-3190)

                           Long Hike: past Cantilever Rock and up to the Chin.          About
                                      6 miles. Leave Montpelier at 10:00 a.m.

                                                       Leader - Doris E. Washburn (476-4264)
                                           ********
    Saturday, July 24    - OVERNIGHT - Leave Montpelier at 10:00 a.m. Starting at
          and              Lake Mansfield Trout Club, to Taylor Lodge for lunch, to
    Sunday, July 25        Puffer Lodge for supper and bedding down. Leave Puffer
                           Lodge after breakfast next morning, lunch at Bolton Lodge,
                           ending hike at Route 2. Bring lunch and supper for
                           Saturday, and breakfast and lunch for Sunday.

                                                       Leader - Dave Morse (3-7126)
                                     *********

                         Up Skylight Pond Trail to the Long Trail. North over

Saturday, August 21    - Leave Montpelier at 9:30 a.m. Climb Hunger Mountain.
                         (Bring pail if interested in picking blueberries.) Others
                         might possibly do some trail clearing from Hunger to Hogback
                         Mountain.

                                                   Leader - King Warburton (3-6486)
                                        ********
Sunday, September 12   - All day hike up Giant Mountain in New York State. Two and
                         one half hour drive to foot of mountain. Beautiful scenery.
                         Bring trail lunch. Leave Montpelier at 8:00 a.m.

                                                   Leader - Dick Babcock (3-7498)
                                                                or
                                                            Dave Otis (3-3190)

UNSCHEDULED - Possible overnight hike on Camel's Hump this summer.

TWO IMPORTANT DATES:
Saturday, May 29       - G.M.C. Inc. Annual Meeting at Long Trail Lodge. Marion
                         Gorham and Harlan Farnsworth are in charge this year and
                         they hope for a good representation from the Montpelier
                         Section. Beginning at 9:30 a.m. - coffee and picnic lunch
                         by the Killington Section. Buffet supper at the Lodge,
                         with speaker, Mr. Perry Merrill. (Charge for supper $3.50)
                         Call Marion Gorham for further details. (3-2674)

Saturday, August 28    - Intersectional at Camp Plymouth in Plymouth.     See the Long
