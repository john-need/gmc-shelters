                                                                          The Cross Vermont Trail
                                                        On June 4 the Cross Vermont Trail Association (CVTA) celebrated the
                                                    completion of a new bridge across the Winooski River, together with an
                                                    extension of the Montpelier Bike Path from Gallison Hill Road to new trail-
                                                    head parking on Route 2. The celebration included the unveiling of a gran-
                                                    ite reproduction of a railroad mile marker that holds memorial plaques for
                                                    people who have been instrumental in the organization’s work. The new
                                                    bridge is a crucial link in CVTA’s growing regional trail network.
                                                        Cross Vermont Trail is a state-wide biking and walking route that high-
                                                    lights local trails and identifies the best roads to use to connect one to
                                                    another. The organization’s long-term goal is to create a complete off-road
route, joining local trails together into a state-wide network that traverses the width of Vermont following the Winooski and
Wells Rivers. For project updates and timelines go to https://crossvermont.org or call 802-498-0079.
     Future area construction plans include a new trail connecting U-32 to the East Montpelier trail, new walking trails along
the banks of the Winooski, and a final extension of the main bike path across East Montpelier to Route 14 where the current
trail begins on the rail bed and continues in various forms east across the state. Trail users should be aware that sections of
the trail still being built will be closed to public access during active construction. Volunteers are always welcome.
