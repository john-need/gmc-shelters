A FEW EASY WAYS TO GO GREEN THAT WILL HARDLY HURT AT ALL
                                                                                                    by Nancy Schulz

  Do you feel overwhelmed by the environmental problems that        face  our world today? If you are awed    by the
-magnitude and complexity of them, the danger is that you may fall into a state of cynical paralysis. Convinced that
 you're not powerful enough to affect change, you probably won't even try.
  If and when you sense yourself slipping into this frame of mind, it’s important to remember that each individual
always has the power to make an impact. It is up to each of us to recognize that we can make a difference and
then (most important
                of all) -            begin!
 Listed below are some simple suggestions that are easy to adopt. Each action on the list will have a positive
impact on your local environment and, by extension, the global environment, Please consider incorporating some
or all of the following into your lifestyle. You'll be making a difference and you'll feel good about doing so!
