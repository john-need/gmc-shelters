FALL SCHEDULE - 1965

MONTPELIER SECTION                                           GREEN MOUNTAIN CLUB


Sunday, September 19     - Family corn roast at Shady Rill Fish =and Game Picnic Area.
                           Bring your own supper; corn and coffee furnished. Leave
                           Montpelier at 4:00 p.m. - Eat at 5:00 p.m.

                           RESERVATIONS NECESSARY       Leader:   Harlan Farnsworth
                                                                  (223-2240)

                          *****************
Saturday, September 25   - A short hike up Spruce Mountain in Plainfield.      Bring trail
                           lunch. Leave Montpelier at 10:00 a.m.

                                                        Leader:   Doris Washburn
                                                                  (476-4264)

                          *****************
Sunday, October 3        - Lincoln Gap to Appalachian Gap, 11.1 miles. Very scenic.
                           Foliage should be at its best. Bring trail lunch. Leave
                           Montpelier at 8:00 a.m.

                                                        Leader:   Marion Gorham
                                                                  (223-2674)

                          *****************
Sunday, October 10       - An afternoon walk - Upper Terrace Street to Route 2.
                           Approximately 2 miles (Hike can be lengthened to approx-
                           imately four miles if group so desires). Leave Montpelier
                           at 1:45 p.m.

                                                        Leader:   Frances Holmes
                                                                  (223-5129

                          *****************
Sunday, October 17       - Our annual trip to the White Mountains. Up Falling Waters
                           Trail, over Liberty Mountain, and down the Flume Slide
                           Trail. Approximately seven miles. Bring trail lunch.
                           Leave Montpelier at 8:00 a.m.

                                                        Leader:   Dave Morse
                                                                  (223-7126)

                          *****************
Saturday, October 30     - Halloween Mystery Hike - "MARSHMALLOWS AT MIDNIGHT". All
                           witches and hobgoblins invited, dress optional. Call
                           leader for further details. Leave Montpelier at 7:30 p.m.

                                                        Leader:   Dave Otis
                                                                  (223-3190)
