                                              Section Annual Meeting
                                          Friday, April 3, 2009, 5:30 P.M.
          We are excited to hold our section annual meeting at the T. W. Wood Gallery, 36 College Street, Mont-
         pelier, on Friday, April 3, 2009. This is a beautiful space, unique to Montpelier, and will be a wonderful
         venue to celebrate the friendships and activities of the Montpelier Section of the Green Mountain Club.
           Lexi Shear will be our guest speaker. Author of The Natural History of the Long Trail, she will hike with
         us up a local mountain (possibly Camel's Hump), talking about the plants and animals we would see on
         the hike, how they live and interact in their communities.
           Lexi has a degree in botany from the Field Naturalist Program at UVM, and has worked as an ecolo-
         gist for the Trust for Public La.nd. A veteran long-distance hiker, she h'a s thru-hiked the 2, 700-mile Pa-
         cific Crest Trail and se.etion hikec:.i Vermont's Long Trail. Her numer0us other hikes include a journey on
         the Salzburger Almenweg with her husband and 12-month old daughter. She lives with her family in
         Montpelier, where she teaches biology and chemistry at Montpelier High School. Lexi has been a mem-
         ber of the Green Mountain Club since 2000 and has led numerous trips and workshops for the club.
         Most recently, she and her husband Dave Blumenthal, have organized the Young Adventurers' Club
         (YAC) for the youngest of club members.
          The meeting starts. at 5:30 P.M .. with a social gathering and potluck supper. The annual business
         meeting will follow (we promise it will be short), and will end with our guest speaker.

                                Planning for 2009 GMC Annual Meeting under Way
         The 2009 Annual Meeting Committee has met twice, and planning for the May 29-31 weekend at the
      new Visitor Center in Waterbury Center is well under way. We have rounded up speakers for Friday
      and Saturday evenings, have planned a variety of outings, and contracted with the New England Culi-
      nary Institute to cater the Saturday dinner. For more information, look for the full program in the Spring
      issue of Long Trail News.

  The rating levels of difficulty are easy, moderate, and difficult, with a few outings falling in between.
        • Easy - accessible to all in good physical condition; suitable for families with children .
        • Moderate - requires a degree of stamina; some previous experience is advisable.
        •Difficult - challenging events for people in good physical condition; previous experience necessary.
  Unless otherwise noted, trips leave from the Montpelier High School (MHS) parking lot. Always bring appropri-
ate gear for the event you are attending (i.e. lunch, liquids, snacks, suitable clothing, footwear, and other items as
mentioned in the Calendar of Events). Non-members are welcome on our events.
  Our trip leaders are volunteers who are not necessarily trained in first aid, nor does the Montpelier Section or
the GMC certify or license trip leaders. Participants should attend these events with the understanding that they
are responsible for their own preparedness and safety, and that all contribute to the well-being of the group.
