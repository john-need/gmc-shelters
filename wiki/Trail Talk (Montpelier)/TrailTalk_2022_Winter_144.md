                   Burnt Rock Hike
    On August 13, a perfect day for hiking, ten of us headed
up the Hedgehog Brook Trail to Burnt Rock Mountain.
Dana Lawrence was the trip leader, and at some point he
suggested we take turns as lead hiker, which was a fun
way to switch things up and chat with different co-hikers
along the way. The two-mile trail is on private land in
Fayston off Big Basin Road. An additional 0.6 miles on
the Long Trail takes you to the Burnt Rock summit, mak-
ing for a round trip total of 5.2 miles.
