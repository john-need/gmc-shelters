         STOWE BOARDWALK OPEN
                               by Andrew Nuquist

  On October 27, the wheelchair accessible boardwalk,
 starting at Barnes Camp on Route 108 in Stowe, was
 officially opened. The ceremony was at Barnes Camp,


                                                                  bers.
                                                                    The boardwalk gives easy access for any
                                                                  wheelchair bound person, through a swamp
                                                                  at the bottom of the Notch to a superior view
                                                                  point up into Smugglersʼ Notch, a viewpoint
                                                                  that will be relished by any hiker or tourist.
                                                                  While the boardwalk ends in one-eighth of a
                                                                  mile, requiring wheelchairs to return to
                                                                  Barnes Camp, early in 2018 the walk will be
                                                                  extended to connect with the Long Trail, and
                                                                  thus will be incorporated into the Long Trail,
                                                                  which will thereafter include the new board-
                                                                  walk. The photo from the wheelchair turn-
                                                                  around point shows an unfinished walkway
                                                                  into the woods and the start of the connec-
                                                                  tion to open the new 2018 LT route.
