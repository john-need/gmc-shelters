APPLAUSE! APPLAUSE!
  For the past two years, Mary Deaett has done the layout for TRAIL TALK. She has chosen the typeface, arranged
the copy on the page, chosen the woodcuts and "fillers," and last spring she designed our new masthead. The
modem look of the newsletter you hold in your hand began with Mary.
  Other commitments have forced Mary to give up this part of her GMC volunteer work. Mary, we thank you for
all the hours you have donated to TRAIL TALK, and for improving it so visibly!
