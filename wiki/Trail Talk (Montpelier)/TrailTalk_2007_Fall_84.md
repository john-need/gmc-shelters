                                       DAVID OTIS MEMORIAL
                                                             by Doris Washburn

  A memorial service for David Otis was held on June 24th at the Green Mount Cemetery in Montpelier. Two of
Dave's antique cars, plus his motorcycle were parked in the cemetery near his stone, a beautiful granite marker
                                                                 with Camel's Hump etched in the background and a
                                                                 couple walking hand-in-hand down the trail toward
                                                                 the mountain from a roadster parked nearby. At the
                                                                 bottom of the stone is a replica of the Green Moun-
                                                                 tain Club logo which Dave designed in 1960. After a
                                                                 committal service led by Rev. Mark Pitton, Bethany
                                                                 United Church of Christ, several people spoke of
                                                                 their remembrances of David. This was followed by
                                                                 the group singing ulong, Long Trail A winding" led
                                                                 by Anthony Otis, Dave's son. Then the Cold Country
                                                                 Bluegrass Band played and sang a few of Dave's
                                                                 favorites.
                                                                   The group then proceeded to the Capitol Plaza,
                                                              c: Montpelier Room, for a scrumptious luncheon of
                                                              ~ Dave's favorite foods highlighted with oyster stew,
                                                              ~ which Dave and Ann served for several years at
                                                              ~Taylor Lodge for the Montpelier Section of the GMC.
                                                                 The menu also included champagne, Cabot cheese,
Manghi breads, Harrington hams, salad green and raw vegetables, "Fruit of the Forest" pie (apple and berries),
Ben & Jerry's ice cream, and maple syrup. During the time of fellowship, pictures were shown of Dave's family,
friends, and highlights of his life. This was a very touching farewell to a true and caring friend.
