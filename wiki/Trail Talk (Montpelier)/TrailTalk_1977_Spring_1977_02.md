                                    OUTING SCHEDULE

                                FEBRUARY 1977 - MAY 1977
      Thia schedule is prepared by the Montpelie ~ Section of the Green Mountain Club,
Inc. which has its headquarters in Rutland, Vernont. Membership in the section
automatically results in membership in the mair. club and in a subscription to
.!h! Long Trail News which is published quarterly. A portion of each section member's
dues is forwarded to the main club .

     While our reason for being is to maintain a section of the Long Trail north of
Smugglers Notch, we schedule a full year of varied outdoor activities for the pleasure
of our members. Guests are always welcome to participate. Outings are planned to
vary in difficulty to suit all abilities. When in doubt, call the leader. Attendance
at the scheduled planning meetings is encouraged as this is where ideas for outings are
generated. Except for meals, advance registration is not required but is always
useful to the leader and to inform you of any last minute changes. Unless otherwise
stated, all trips will meet at the Montpelier High School parking lot. Officers and
dues are listed at the end of this schedule.
