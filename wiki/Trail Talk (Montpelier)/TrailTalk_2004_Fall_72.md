         • Easy - are accessible to all in good physical condition; suitable for families with children.

         • Difficult - challenging events for people in good physical condition; previous experience necessary.

event you are attending (i.e. lunch, liquids, snacks, suitable clothing, footwear, bike helmet, and other items as mentioned in
