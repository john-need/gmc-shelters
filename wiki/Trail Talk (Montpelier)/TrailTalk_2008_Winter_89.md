            The Montpelier Section will sponsor a talk/slide show by Deborah Lewis of Bredeson Outdoor Adven-
           tures, of a trip she led this past summer. We will travel vicariously with her on foot from France to Italy
                                               to Switzerland and back to France. The Tour du Mont Blanc (or TMB) ,
                                                a circuit hike around Mont Blanc, Europe's highest mountain at almost
                                               sixteen thousand feet, is one of the world's classic hikes. In this mid-
                                                mountain circuit, travelers spend each night in a different location as
                                               they hike around the base of this white, glacier-covered massif. This
                                               spectacular trek passes by charming villages, near glaciers and even
                                               on a stretch of an old Roman road. It is also a journey back in time,
                                               since its traditional base, Chamonix, is the birthplace of mountaineer-
                                                ing in the Alps. While challenging, the TMB also has its creature com-
                                        ~-- forts, since hikers usually stay in comfortable hotels, guesthouses, and
                                               the occasional high mountain hut, offering good food and camarade-
                                                rie. Soft beds and hard hikes is a good way to sum up this adventure.
                                                  Join us on Friday, January 16, 7:00 P.M. at the Capitol City Grange
                                                in Montpelier. From the intersection of Main Street and Memorial
                                      ~_,._,Drive, travel 1 mile on Rte. 12 (Northfield Street). Just past the
           1-89 overpass, turn left and go up the driveway. Refreshments will be on sale before and during in-
           term ission. Admission is $5.00 members, $8.00 non-members, children under 12 are free.

                                   Section to Host 2009 GMC Annual Meeting
              The Montpelier Section has the honor once again of hosting the Green Mountain Club's annual meet-
           ing on May 29-31 , 2009, in the club's new "barn," now under construction , on Route 100 in Waterbury
           Center.
              In 1993, after the GMC's 1992 move from Montpelier to Route 100, our section served as host for the
           first annual meeting held at club headquarters in Waterbury Center. We now want to make the 2009
           meeting another memorable event for members who want to see our new building.
              The Montpelier Section's Executive Committee has formed a 2009 Annual Meeting Committee that
           has begun to plan next spring's festivities. We are identifying many tasks for section volunteers at the
           time of the meeting, and will be soliciting your help in the coming months. Please tell us if you will ac-
           cept an assignment during the meeting, or would like to assume a planning role on the committee,
           which will be meeting through the winter and early spring. We will be working on registration, set up
           and clean up, meals, programs, outings, and lodging.
              Ann Burcroff is coordinating overnight accommodations for attendees, and she would like to include
           private housing with Montpelier Section members. Both hosts and guests will benefit from the opportu-
           nity to make or renew friendships among GMC members. Ann is gathering names of section members
           willing to house a guest during the meeting. Please tell Ann if can accept a guest in your home next
           May. You can reach her at 229-9677 or aburcroff@vtlink.net.

  The rating levels of difficulty are easy, moderate, and difficult, with a few outings falling in between.
        • Easy - accessible to all in good physical condition; suitable for families with children.
        • Moderate - requires a degree of stamina; some previous experience is advisable.
        •Difficult - challenging events for people in good physical condition; previous experience necessary.
  Unless otherwise noted, trips leave from the Montpelier High School (MHS) parking lot. Always bring appropri-
ate gear for the event you are attending (i.e. lunch, liquids, snacks, suitable clothing, footwear, and other items as
mentioned in the Calendar of Events). Non-members are welcome on our events.
  Our trip leaders are volunteers who are not necessarily trained in first aid, nor does the Montpelier Section or
the GMC certify or license trip leaders. Participants should attend these events with the understanding that they
are responsible for their own preparedness and safety, and that all contribute to the well-being of the group.
