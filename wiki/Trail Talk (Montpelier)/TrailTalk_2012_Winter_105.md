     COME JOIN US!
       The Montpelier Section Taylor Lecture Series presentation, "The Green Mountain Parkway Myth", will
     be held on Friday, January 18, 2013 at 7:00 P.M. at the Capital City Grange in Montpelier. Cookies
     and cider will be on sale before the event and during intermission.
                                                                                On its surface the 1936 defeat of
                                                                              the proposed Green Mountain
                                                                              Parkway was a triumph for the
                                                                              environmental movement. But to
                                                                              Vermont historian Bruce Post, this
                                                                              view vastly oversimplifies a much
                                                                              more complex subject.
                                                                                Through analysis of actual park-
                                                                              way plans and land development
                                                                              since the proposal's defeat.• Post
                                                                              will argue that in many ways our
                                                                              state's natural treasures would
                                                                              have been better protected had
                                                                              the road actually been built.
                                                                                Join us for a stimulating revision-
     ist view of what Post calls "The Green Mountain Parkway Myth."
            Admission: $5.00 for GMC members; $8.00 for non-members; children under 12 are free.
       Montpelier directions: From the intersection of Main Street and Memorial Drive, travel 1 mile south
     on Rte. 12 (Northfield Street). Just past the 1-89 overpass, turn left and go up the driveway.

                                 SECTION DIRECTOR'S REPORT
                                                     by Mike Wetherell
  A beautiful morning created a sunny backdrop for GMC's September 22, 2012 board meeting and for-
tunately the dark clouds held off the rain as 150+ volunteers from all over Vermont and all parts of the
club enjoyed an afternoon of good food and fellowship.
  President Jean Haigh opened the meeting by sharing vignettes of her many outings with GMC trail
crews over the summer and recognizing staff members for the contributions to the summer field season.
  Will Wiquist's reported these highlights:
    •that the club still has over 10,000 members and 85 business members,
    • noted that GMC is revising the 50 Hikes book,
    • section members Matt and Alyssa Krebs are working on a book on hiking with kids,
    • improvements to financial management,
    • and GMC's positive audit results.
  Dave Hardy provided updates on the Winooski Bridge, an opportunity to manage Bolton Lodge and
Bryant Camp, Thundering Falls, changes since Irene, the Cold River relocation, North Country Scenic
Trail legislation, and other field season activities. GMC signed a bridge contract with VHB, and GMC is
moving through the permitting process, which is currently focused on the Development Review Board.
There's a great video on the bridge project featuring Daan Zwick that members can view on YouTube.
The Trails Management Committee will review and make recommendations on the Bolton and Bryant
developments. Pete Antos-Ketcham added that the Appalachian Trail in Vermont has been completely
flagged.
  The next GMC board meeting is in January, and the next annual meeting will be on June 81 2013, at
Stratton Mountain.
  The Montpelier Section funded wireless microphone was put to good use. Thanks everyone!
