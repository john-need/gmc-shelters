ALLIS TRAIL 8£NCH DEDICATION
                                                          by Doris Washburn

      Under sunny skies, with our minds full of good memories of David Morse, we started our climb at the
    Couching Lion Fann site to the Allis trail. Along the
   way we stopped at Hump Brook and the Beaver Pond
   to relive our own private recollections. Soon we
   reached the Allis trail and found that someone had
   just clipped the overgrown branches. The fragrance of
   fresh cut spruce was as perfect as the view for the
    completion and dedication of the bench. Bob
   Lindemann built the bench from trees in the area. We
   thank you Bob for all your generous work. This
   bench was dedicated to the memory of the late David
   Morse who loved this area as a trail adopter for the
   GMC for many years.

   on a tree in back of the bench and spoke of David's
