                                                        Section Membership
                                                            by Heather Bailey
   There are perks to becoming a section member as opposed to an at-large member of the Green Mountain Club. You
   have a local community of hiking enthusiasts to go out on a trail with. You have a subscription to Trail Talk, which
   has articles on good places to hike, paddle or bike. It keeps you abreast of what’s going on in our section. You have
   the opportunity to become more involved by becoming a member of the executive committee. The committee meets
   several times a year and plans events such as work hikes, the annual meeting, and special events such as Phyllis
   Rubinstein’s recent presentation of her Appalachian Trail hike. You could also become an outing leader and share your
   knowledge of the trail with others.
