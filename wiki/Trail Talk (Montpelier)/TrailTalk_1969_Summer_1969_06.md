MONTPELIER SECTION
GREEN MOUNTAIN CLUB                     SCHEDULE                      JUNE-AUGUST 1969
******************************************
Sunday, June 8th - Trail clearing in the Smuggler's Notch area - north on the Long
                   Trail and possibly the Elephant's Head Trail. Bring lunches,
                   axes, clippers, etc. We need plenty of help on this trip.
                   Group will meet at 8:00 a.m. at the Montpelier High School
                   parking lot. Contact leader for further details.

                   LEADER - Jack Denton - Tel. 223-2679

******************************************
Sunday, June 22nd - All day hike in the Camel's Hump area. We plan to go up the
                   Burrows Trail, over the top of Camel's Hump via the Long Trail,
                   down to Montclair Glen, and out the Forest City Trail. Bring
                   trail lunch. Call leader for further details.

                   LEADER - Doris Washburn - Tel. 476-4264

******************************************
Weekend of July 4th - Camping and Canoeing.    Open to suggestions.    Contact leaders.

                   LEADERS:   Dick and Dot Babcock - Tel. 223-7498

******************************************
Sunday, July 13th - Grandview Mountain located in Addison. We should find inter-
                   esting flora on this mountain, including pitcher plants. Call
                   leaser for details.
                   LEADER - Dave Otis   - Tel. 223-3190

******************************************
Sunday, July 27th - An all day trip to climb Mt, Hale in New Hampshire. Thl.s
                   mountain in 4, 077 feet high. There will be both a long (about
                   ten miles) and a short hike here. Call leaders for more details.

                   LEADERS .. George and Cynthia Brady : - Tel. 479-9598

******************************************
Sunday, August 10th - A hiking trip to Heart Lake in the Adirondacks. We will meet
                   the Babcocks at Adirondack Loj at Heart take and climb Algonquin
                   and Wright, Call leader for more details.

                   LEADER - Dave Morse - Tel . 223-7126

******************************************
Wednesday, August 20th - Next Planning Meeting at the Dentons in Montpelier. We
                   welcome new ideas for trips. If anyone would like to do certain
                   sections of the Long Trail, we would be glad to schedule a hike
                   to suit them.

******************************************
DON'T FORBET THE INTER.SECTIONAL AT CAMP WIHAKOWI IN ROXBURY BEGINNING SATURDAY,
AUGUST 23RD AND CONTINUING THROUGH SEPTEMBER lST.   WE NEED VOLUNTEER HELP DCMN THERE 11
