         FALL/WINTER SAFETY
                       Be Prepared!
  Before heading out on a fall/winter hike, it may be a
good idea to read pages 18-20, and 21-23 in the new
Twenty-Sixth Edition of the Long Trail Guide. One
should always be prepared for all types of weather. A
small first aid kit, a loud whistle, and a space blanket
co'uld save your life .

greenmountainclub.org, click on the GMC Store link,
click on Books, or purchase at the GMC headquarters
in Waterbury Center, 802-244-7037.
