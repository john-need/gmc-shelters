MONTPELIER SECTION                                        GREEN MOUNTAIN CLUB
******************************************
Sunday, November 13th      Trail Clearing - Let's try to finish up the work
                           we started last spring (when there was too much
                           snow left on the trail). We will be working on
                           the section of the Long Trail from Smugglers
                           Notch to Chilcoot Pass, also on the Sterling
                           Pond Lodge. Bring trail lunch.

                           For details, call Dave Morse ·(3-7126)

                         ******************
Sunday, November 27th      An all-day hike up the Haselton Trail to the
                           top of the toll road on Mt. Mansfield, a dis-
                           tance of two miles. Those interested may
                           explore some of the side trails on top of the
                           mountain. Bring trail lunch, and possibly
                           snowshoes will be needed.

                           For details, call Dave Otis (3-3190)

                         ******************
Sunday, December 11th      You are cordially invited to a Christmas
                           Party at Marion's home {RSVP, please) . Come
                           early, anytime after supper (6:30-7:00) and
                           bring snowshoes, skis, sleds or toboggan, and
                           a flashlight - - - or come later (8:00-9:00)
                           for refreshments and conversation - - - - -
                           b u t    c o me. Hot buttered rum for imbibers;
                           non-alcoholic punch for teetotalers and youngsters.

                           For further details, call Marion Gorham (3-2674)

                         ******************
Tuesday, December 27th     Moonlight snowshoe hike in the Barre Country
                           Club area, Bring flashlight.

                           For details, call Doris E. Washburn (476-4264)

                         ******************
