                                     Two Weeks in Patagonia
		 On March 16th Montpelier Section member Scott Richardson will give a presentation about his
time in Patagonia. The event is at 6:30 PM at the Kellogg-Hubbard Library. Two Weeks in Patagonia will
give audience members a visual journey through the dramatic landscapes of southern Patagonia, from the
iconic granite spires of Torres del Paine to the jagged skyline of Fitz Roy and Cerro Torre. Included will
be an inside look at the logistics and practical trail information relevant to trekking in Tierra del Fuego.


                              Montpelier Section Annual Meeting
     The GMC Montpelier Section’s Annual Meeting and potluck is scheduled
for Friday, May 1. The presentation this year will be a recording of State Archi-
vist Reidun Nuquist’s talk Green Mountain Girls: Women of the Long Trail.
Reidun was one of the longest-serving members of the Montpelier section and
of the Green Mountain Club overall. Her talk is a fascinating look at the history
of women in the Green Mountains starting in 1914 and continuing into the 21st
century. (See page 2 for details.)
