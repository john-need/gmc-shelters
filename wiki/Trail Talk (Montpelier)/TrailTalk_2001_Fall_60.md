• Easy - outings are accessible to all in good physical condition; suitable for families with children.

• Difficult - challenging events for people in good physical condition; previous experience is necessary.

appropriate gear for the event you are attending (i.e. lunch, liquids, snacks, suitable clothing, footwear,

A HUGE SUCCESS
                                                   by Priscilla Page

  The GMC's Annual Meeting at Bolton Valley Resort on June 8, 9, 1011i was very successful due in
large part to the location, the cooperation of the weatherman, and all the Section members who
hosted the event. A few statistics from the weekend: 147 registered (this number includes GMC
staff); 21 hiked on Friday afternoon; 45 attended the Nuquists' Friday evening slideshow (this attests
to their reputation for excellent photography and travelogue); approximately 72 participated in the
Saturday afternoon hikes, and 9 attended the work trip on Sunday morning. The raffle netted
$145.00 for the Club.
  At the business meeting, Andrew & Reidun Nuquist received the Presidents Award for their many
years of volunteered quality service. Ten Year Service Awards were given to: Eric Seidel, Bill
Osgood, Peter Downey, Mary Jo Llewelyn, and Dave Shepard. Five Year Service Awards went to:
Christie Carter, Seth Coffey, Cindy Lindemann, Duncan Wilkie, and Roger Whitcomb.
  Thank you to the following Section members who helped with planning the events or participated
in the weekend's activities: John Buddington*, Ann Burcro:ff*, Christie Carter*, Michael Chernick*,
Priscilla Daggett, Roberta Downey, Manny and Mary Garcia, Mark Haughwout, Joan Heller, Fred*
and Nancy* Jordan, Ray* and Sylvia Kingsbury, Steve Lightholder, Cindy Lindemann*, Betty
Moskowitz, Judy Murray, Reidun* and Andrew Nuquist, Eric Seidel, Doris Washburn*, Thomas.
Weiss, and Duncan Wilkie.
*indicates Planning Committee Members
    In a letter thanking the Section for hosting the meeting, GMC President Marty Lawthers, writes,
"On behalf of the entire Green Mountain Club, I would like to thank the Montpelier Section for a
wonderful annual meeting. I know it was a team effort so please pass my thanks on to all who
worked so hard to put on the event." And she ends her note, "It is volunteers .....that makes the
GMC so special."
