Sunday, December 1st - Blue Ridge Mountain. This 3,~95 foot mountain is located to
the west of the Long Trail and north of Rt. 4 near Rutland. The Green Mountain Club
maintains the 2.4 mile Canty Trail which begins on the Old Turnpike Road one-half
mile from Route 4. The trail crosses several brooks. From the summit may be had
extensive views of Pico and other peaks of the Coolidge Range, Stratton, Dorset,
Herrick and Bird Mountains nearby, and the Adirondacks on the horizon. Bring a
trail lunch and possibly snowshoes may be needed .
