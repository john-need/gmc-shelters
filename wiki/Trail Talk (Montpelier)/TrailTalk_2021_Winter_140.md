October 3, 2021 – Barre, Millstone Quarries hike
by Steve and Heather Bailey
  The weather forecast was for 50% chance of rain,
which turned out to be the percentage of people
who actually came to the hike compared to those
who signed up. We had 9 hikers out of 17 who had
thought they might come. It was raining on the way
to the trailhead, and drizzling in the parking lot, but
miraculously it stopped just before we started hik-
ing and didn't rain for the three hours or so of hik-
ing. We hiked in a loop around the Rock of Ages
quarry, starting from the old (now abandoned)
Rock of Ages Visitor Center on Graniteville Road in
Graniteville, Barre Town. The foliage was not at its
peak, but we saw lots of beautiful scenery. We had
to pass through part of the active quarry on our
loop, so it was good that it was Sunday and no one
was working there.

PRESIDENT’S MESSAGE
                                                      by Dana Lawrence

                        As the warmth of our extended fall slides into the inevitable wonders
                      of winter, I hope our members are well and enjoying this holiday sea-
                      son in its spirit.
                               For though the weather may be sometimes frightful,
                               And the fire so delightful,
                               There’s always a place to go,
                               Let it snow, let it blow,
                               You can still go!
                        Yes, it’s time to bundle up and get out of the house. Check out our
                      outing calendar, connect with friends old and new, and enjoy the win-
                      ter wonderland that is Vermont.
                        We are looking to add events to our calendar as weather and addi-
                      tional outing volunteer leaders come forward, so look for new an-
                      nouncements on our website calendar and,Front Porch Forum in your
                      area.
                        So it’s time to get out skis and spikes, hats and mittens, snowshoes
                      and winter boots. The woods, roads, and trails are calling.
                        See you on the trail.
