landscapes depicted in the 19th-century
publication "Picturesque America." This
publication, released in the 1870s, featured
stunning engravings and narratives that
highlighted the beauty of American landscapes
and raised awareness about the need for
conservation. Varn's initiative seeks to continue
this legacy by encouraging contemporary
artists to revisit these sites and create modern
renditions, thereby fostering a deeper
appreciation for these locations and their
preservation.

His organization reached out to the University
of Vermont and the Green Mountain Club as
part of their activity to visit and identify
picturesque views associated with Mount
Mansfield in the original publication.

PAPA organizes various events, including art
shows and fundraisers, to support conservation
efforts. The organization collaborates with local
artists, historians, and environmental groups to
promote awareness about the importance of
preserving America's picturesque landscapes.
PAPA's efforts not only highlight the beauty of
these sites but also encourage community
involvement in conservation initiatives.

Waterbury Backcountry Rescue was called out
at 6:37 PM on a report of four hikers trapped
by darkness attempting to descend the Dean
