THE HAUNTED FOREST
Tess Stimson

  Hallowe’en this year saw a few
intrepid Burlington section hikers
venture into the “haunted forests” on
Camel’s Hump, with buckets of
treats and plenty of Hallowe’en spirit.
  Among them was visiting hiker
Cynde Strand, an Emmy-award-
winning media executive and highly
experienced journalist with CNN.
  Cynde returned to Vermont this fall
for the second year to teach
journalism at St. Michael’s College in
Colchester.
  She joined the GMC and has hiked
nearly every weekend — most of
them properly dressed.
  For Hallowe’en, she took a bucket
of treats with her and passed them
out on the trail, much to the delight
of fellow hikers.                                                Above) Cynde “Dead Cat” Strand
                                                            (Below) Handing out treats on the trail
