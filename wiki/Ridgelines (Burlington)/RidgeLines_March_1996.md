                                        A CARETAKER                 FOR ALL SEASONS
             In May 1993 a former caretaker with a special connection to Taft Lodge wrote to us: "I would like to propose ... that a
     study be made of what is needed to bring Taft up to a decent quality of life in its old age. ... I could make a decision to pay for
     the materials and other expenses needed to do a good job."
             The formen~aretaker was Daan Zwick, a long-time Burlington Section GMC'er, whose alliance with Taft Lodge began
     when he was seven and has never ended. He was a caretaker at Taft in 1939-41. In the fifties he joined work parties, and in
     the nineties, he has been a "Volunteer Summit Caretaker" for the GMC on Mansfield. It was after one of his visits in 1993 that
     he wrote "I have been acutely aware that Taft Lodge needs repair."
            Taft Lodge, the GMC's oldest shelter (1920), also its largest and most used, is located on the east side of Mt.
     Mansfield. Following Daan's letter and in part in response to it, a committee within the GMC was formed. They made the first
     of many inspection trips to the Lodge in October 1993. Readers of this newsletter are familiar with the step by step work of the
     committee since then to restore Taft Lodge. (See Dana Baron's latest report in this issue.)
             In the following paragraphs Daan shares memories and thoughts about Taft Lodge.
