                                                            Lorne Currier, Volunteer and Education Coordinator
                                                            at the GMC provided this response:

                                                            GMC considers all aspects of the trail environment
                                                            and uses our Long Trail System Management Plan
                                                            to answer questions like yours about trail design.
                                                            The GMC emphasizes simplicity, sustainability and
                                                            self-reliance, in design and construction of the Long
                                                            Trail System. One of the questions we ask is: “Does
                                                            this action unnecessarily sacrifice aspects of the
                                                            trail that provide solitude or reduce the primitive
                                                            quality of the Trail?”

                                                            We also consider:
                                                            • Natural resources impact
                                                            • Primitive and challenging hiking experience
                                                            • Inherent risk in backcountry

                                                            While steel rungs have been added in some areas,
                                                            Mansfield is well known and well respected for its
                                                            rugged, primitive and sometimes harrowing envi-
                                                            ronment. The forehead and the chin of Mansfield
                                                            are often people’s favorite, most memorable part of
                                                            the Long Trail, and installing more aids in that area
                                                            may detract from the primitive quality the Long Trail
                                                            seeks to provide. That differs from Theron Dean,
                                                            where there was a very clear natural resource impact
                                                            and little alternative relocation options, so at the
                                                            time they chose to add aids such as rungs.

Butler Lodge Loop

Mansfield Ridge. A pretty but moderately difficult
