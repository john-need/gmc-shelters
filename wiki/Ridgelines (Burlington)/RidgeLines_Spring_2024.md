the dedication ceremony for the newly built
Peru Peak shelter in 2000, but lucky for all he
happened-by while out on an afternoon hike.
Always up for a photo-op, Dean conducted an
impromptu ribbon-cutting using a roll of tape
and an ax.

Governor Dean is an avid hiker and remains,
to date, the only Vermont Governor to hike the
