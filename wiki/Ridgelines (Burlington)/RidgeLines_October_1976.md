       Green Mountain Club members were sad-
 dened last summer by the passing of Julia
 Hatch, one of our loyal long-time members.
 Her way of life was truly an enviable rapport
 with God's children as well as His wonders
 of nature.   Her keen· intellect, readiness to
 help, and dedication to worthy causes were
 qualities for which she will long be remem-
 bered.    Her spirit of adventure and undaunted
 courage should forever be an inspiration to us.
 We are all richer for having been her friend.
