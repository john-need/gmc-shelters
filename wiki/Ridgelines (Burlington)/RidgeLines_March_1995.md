                        PROPOSED RESTRUCTURING OF GMC BOARD
            The Burlington Section Executive Committee on Feb. 28 endorsed the following proposal for restructuring the GMC
    Board of Directors. This proposal was drawn up by an advisory group composed of members of the Burlington Section and
    other Sections. It is being presented to the Organizational Review Committee (ORC) and the Board of Directors for
    consideration.
            As many of you are aware, for the past year the ORC has been working with consultant Debbie Bergh to consider
    changes in the structure of the Club. Though a number of the changes proposed by the ORC were approved at the January
    Directors meeting, changes affecting the Board of Directors, membership policies, and dues structure have not yet been
    approved.
            Comments on this recommendation or on any other organizational recommendations are welcomed. To compare this
    proposal with the ORC recommendation, see The Long Trail News, Winter 1994.

  THANK YOU, OUTING LEADERS
          Organizing and leading hikes often involves work and responsibility. It involves little things too: a leader may make
sure a new person has a jacket for the summit, or have moleskin handy when it Is needed, or point out flowers and birds along
the way. A leader has a certain responsibility for the group, and, at the same time, needs not to be authoritarian or to make the
trip feel regimented.
          I'd like to express my appreciation, and that of other hike participants, to those who give their time to take this
responsibility. I am sure we are all grateful to the leaders even if it sometimes seems that we take them for granted.
          To all those who have led an outing, or who have volunteered to lead several, thank you.
                                                                                                                              Dot Myer
