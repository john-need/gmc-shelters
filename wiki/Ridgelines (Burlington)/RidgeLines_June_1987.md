Sept.'!:]__.   Green River Reservoir   Canoe Trip.
        Last year we enjoyed a wonderful fall day at Green River Reservoir.         The Reservoir
        is a lovely lake just north of Morrisville,      VT. It is quite accessible    from
        Burlington.    In September, you can expect a quiet day of canoeing with generally
        cool temperatures.     Light rain does not cancel as this can be the best weather for
        fall colors.    This is one of the few lakes that has virtually    no cabins or camps,
        so solitude  is usually to be found there.
        This should be an ideal day for fall color viewing and checking to see which birds
        have not left town yet.     Bring warm clothing and mittens along with a lunch and
        something warm to drink.     PFD's are required,   so don't leave home without yours.
        Rosemarie Bagioni, Tom Brooks, leaders,     899-2048.
