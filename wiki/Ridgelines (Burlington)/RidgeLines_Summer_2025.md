Section Presidents Message
The snow has melted, mostly, the mud has
started to dry as we approach the summer
hiking season across the Long Trail system. I
am deferring most of my message to the fine
work of our section’s trail volunteers who
completed their annual work of clearing
blowdowns, water bars and clipping to keep
the trails accessible to everyone! This is
strenuous work requiring significant time
commitments. See the highlights from our trails
co-chair Scott Albertson below. See you at
Barnes Camp or out on the trails.

Brian Williams
Burlington Section President
