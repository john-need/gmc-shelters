                                                                                         The Gear
                     ASK SHAZEN!
                                                                        1. NO COTTON: all synthetics or wool.
                                                                        2. Jacket and pants: three-layer Gore-Tex shell
                                                                   if you are going to keep doing this. Otherwise start
                                                                   with something less expensive.
                                                                        3. Boots and gloves/mittens: Gore-Tex or
                                                                   other waterproof breathable materials.
                                                                        4. Snowshoes: Unless you are over 200 lbs. get
                                                                   the shortest, lightest versions, making sure the
                                                                   binding will hold up and is easy to use. No longer
                                                                   than 24” (you’ll be carrying these on your backpack
                                                                   a lot of the time).
                                                                        5. Trail crampons (also known as microspikes):
                                                                   These are a must for the East. The original Kotoola
                                                                   and Hillsound Trail Crampon will be the best t to
                                                                   start with. There are many versions out there now
                                                                   with longer and shorter spikes. The shorter the

                             The Joy Of Ice!

              We all know that ice on the ground can make
          winter travel di cult, though microspikes or
          snowshoes with good traction can help. But ice
          formations on the cli s and rocks can also provide
          some amazing winter scenery.
              My favorite spot is along the Long Trail on
          Laraway, before you reach Laraway lookout as you
          approach it from the south.
              The cli s in this area are slabs of rock that lean
          out from the mountain, and water owing over them
          creates huge freestanding ice columns and walls.
              Another spot with some nice ice is around two
          thirds of the way up Halfway House trail on Mt
          Mans eld. There is a rock wall there that can look
