GENERAL INFORMATION
    Hikers are urged to write to the Green Moun-
tain Club, Inc., 108 Merchants Row, Rutland, Vt.
for the latest information on trails and shelters.
At the beginning of every hiking season parts of
the Long Trail, some side trails, and several shel-
ters may be in poor condition. Listed below are
tho5e trails and! shelters which have been aban-
doned, and those which will probably not be in
good repair in early 1955. Also, some statements
in the Guide Book which have become obsolete
are called to your attention.
    The Official Highway Map of Vermont, a large
scale map of the state showing the Long Trail,
State and Federal Camping Areas, and other fea-
tures of interest to hikers, may be had by writing
to the Vermont Development Commission, Mont-
pelier, Vt. It is available at information booths, etc.
    The Rutland R.R. referred to throughout the
Guide has abandoned passenger service. Bus trans-
portation along its route is provided by the Ver-
mont Transit Co., which also operates elsewhere
in the State. Address: Burlington, Vt.
