     IN MEMORY OF BARBARA VAN WINKELEN
         October 25, 1919 - March 7, 2010

Barbara, who was a long-time member of the Connecticut
Section of the Green Mountain, passed away on March 7,
2010, at the age of 90 years. She spent her last 30 summers at
her Art Gallery on Nantucket Island, Massachusetts. In 1995,
she illustrated a children’s book based on the Old Carousel
Horse in the garden of the Chanticleer Restaurant in
Sciasconset, Nantucket.

Barbara is listed in Who’s Who in American Art. This well-
