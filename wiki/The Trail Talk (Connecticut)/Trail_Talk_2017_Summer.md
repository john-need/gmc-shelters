                                              President's Message
                                                     Fall 2017


As I write this, a group of 12 Connecticut Section members and old friends are in Iceland, on a multi-day hiking trip.
We are staying at houses rented through Air BNB and going on day trips in the south, southwest and western areas
of the island. While the trip has just started, the best description of the scenery is desolate yet beautiful and
spectacular. On our first day we took a glacier walk with a multinational guide who was very informative and
entertaining. We have all probably heard the glaciers are constantly changing and moving but I didn't have a full
appreciation of those phenomena until we were actually standing on an outer arm ofVantnajokull Glacier, Iceland' s
largest. An amazing mix of ice, dirt, rocks and other debris that is scraped and ground up as the glacier advances
and retreats. Over the long term Iceland' s Glaciers are retreating as they are in many places in the world.
Closer to home we are heading into fall when some of the best hiking in Connecticut and the Northeast occurs.
Hopefully we' ll have a fantastic foliage year. Some forecasters are saying it will be better than average. VP of
Activities Dennis Himes has put together our fall and early winter schedule included in this newsletter. Of course in
the fall we share more of the outdoors with hunters, so it' s always wise to wear blaze orange and check the hunting
season and locations information on the respective state websites prior to venturing out.
As a final note our long time newsletter editor Carol A. Langley has communicated her plans to step down at the end
of2017. Carol has been editor since June 1984 then after a well deserved break took up the role again a number of
years ago. We are looking for one, two or a small group to pick up the production of the newsletter which is our
most widely distributed vehicle for communication.

See you on the trail!
Jim Robertson
