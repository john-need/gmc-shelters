                                         IN MEMORY

                    During the first week of November the Killington Section lost two
              longtime members.
                    Car 1 Parsons and Ann Reynolds will be remembered by the longtime
              members for being active during the 1950s through the 1970s.
                    Each and every member expresses his deepest sympathy to the families
              of Carl and Ann.
                   A small gift has been given to the Alzheimer's Research Group in
              memory of Carl Parsons and to the Cancer Society for Mrs. George (Ann)
              Reynolds in hopes that these afflictions will be overcome in time.
