                                       NAME CHANGE COMMITTEE
The Name Change Committee, made up of Iris Berezin-chair, Kathy Astrauckas, Peter Hope, Nick Krembs,
Lynne Miller and Heinz Trebitz, was formed following the last Annual Meeting, with the charge to
 “Consider the advisability of a change to the name of the Section, taking into account any comments from
Section members, and to report its findings at the next annual meeting of the Section; and, if the committee
judges a change might be beneficial, to propose one or more new names at the next annual meeting of the
Section”.

The early direction of the committee has been to explore ways of improving our visibility rather than focusing
specifically on the name of the Section. To that end we have identified and started a number of activities:

        Communicating with GMC headquarters about improving their website,
        Optimizing our website and our website search engine,
        Collecting demographic information about our membership,
        Expanding our use of social media (Facebook),
        Posting outings on the Upper Valley Listserv, in neighboring towns and the UV
        Daily; Using email lists to post outings,
        Contacting weekly newspapers in local towns about how to post outing,
        Exploring the possibility of identifying our section of the Appalachian Trail
        using signs bearing our name,
        Collecting information from outing leaders regarding how non-members found
        out about the outing.
Please contact Iris at irisberezin@myfairpoint.net if you have other ideas or expertise that you would like to share
with the committee.

BOOTS TO BOATS – THE NEXT CHAPTER

        Last year’s “Boots to Boats” collaboration with Marsh-Billings-Rockefeller National Historical Park
was so successful that the program will be repeated this summer and fall. The Ottauquechee Section will
again co-lead short to moderate length hikes on the Appalachian Trail between Woodstock and Norwich with
rangers from the National Park. These hikes are particularly good for beginners and families, because the pace
is relaxed and will include stops at points of interest.
The Park Service plans to arrange a shuttle bus to and from the trailhead, eliminating the need to carpool.
The section will also co-lead a beginner’s trail maintenance outing with the National Park Service on the
Appalachian Trail on Quimby Mountain in Killington.
Outings Coordinator Kathy Astrauckas will contact possible leaders soon to start scheduling the hikes.
(Planning meeting for summer outings is March 14). So far the dates are: June 17; July 1, 15, 29; Aug. 12;
Sept. 2, 16, 30. The program will culminate with a “Peak to Peak” event, probably on Oct. 14.
Feel free to contact Kathy before the planning meeting to secure your preferred dates and hikes.

                            AN INVITATION TO JOIN THE GREEN MOUNTAIN CLUB
Who are we? Founded in 1910, the Green Mountain Club was established "to make the mountains of Vermont play larger roles in
the lives of people". To meet that goal, the club constructed the 272-mile Long Trail (LT). The GMC is responsible for
developing policies and programs aimed at preservation, maintenance, and proper use of hiking trails for the benefit of the people of
Vermont.

What is the Ottauquechee Section? Our section, one of 14 chapters of the GMC, is located in and around the Upper Valley. We
schedule activities all year round for those who enjoy the outdoors, including hikes, bike trips, canoe outings, cross country skiing
and snow shoe hikes. The "O" Section is responsible for the maintenance of 46 miles of Appalachian Trail (AT) between Maine
Junction at Willard Pass near Killington and the Connecticut River at Norwich, VT, including four trail shelters.

We invite you to join! If you wish to participate in our activities, meet friendly, outdoors loving people and contribute to the
maintenance and quality of Vermont's hiking trail system, the Ottauquechee Section welcomes you. Through the quarterly GMC
Long Trail News and the "O"Section Footnotes you will get information about section and main club activities and issues of interest.
For more information, call Patty Spencer at 802-333-3571.

Waterbury Center, VT 05677, att. Kristin McLane. Please enclose a check, payable to the Green Mountain Club.
