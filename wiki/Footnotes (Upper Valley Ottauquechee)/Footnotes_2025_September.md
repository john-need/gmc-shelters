                             President’s Message - Summer 2025

  Summer 2025 began with a large contingent from the UVO Section traveling to GMC Headquarters in
Waterbury Center on June 14th for the Club’s Annual Meeting. What a treat to see the new GMC
Visitors Center and mingle with staff, directors, and fellow section members! We received new insights
in the Club’s efforts to protect and conserve the Long Trail corridor especially in the northern sections of
the trail. It was my special treat to ride along with Past President Inge Brown, who truly delighted in
sharing this day with all of us.
  Sadly, Inge’s health declined. She embarked on her next great adventure on her own terms on 8/1/25.
We will always miss this splendid woman. Please see the information for making Memorial Gifts to the
UVO Section in this issue. This is something she wanted.

                                                  Our spring and early summer trail work involved
                                                clearing more blow downs, large and small, than
                                                anyone can remember. Enormous thanks to our UVO
                                                Sawyers who put in more work than ever before.
                                                  Also, The US Forest Service did a marvellous job
                                                resurfacing the parking lot at Rte. 12 and providing
                                                level footed access to the bridge across Gulf Stream.
                                                See photo on left provided by Seth Coffey.
                                                  We have had some turnover in Trail and Shelter
                                                Adopters. Dick Ruben, thank you for many years of
                                                maintaining Thistle Hill! The new Adopter for Thistle
                                                Hill Shelter is Karen McNall. We also thank Rick
                                                Windish for your years of GMC support. Rick is turning
over Pomfret Rd to Cloudland Rd to Kristen Pizarro and Michael Prosalik. These new Adopters will do
a great job!
  Our summer Outings have perked along with mostly excellent weather. I can attest that my outing to
Camel’s Hump on June 29, (rescheduled from a rainy June 28) was a fabulous day with UVO hikers
joined by hikers from CT and other sections. The refurbished Burrows Trail is a marvel!
  The GMC Governance Review continues. I will supply more information on its progress at the Sept
2nd Quarterly Meeting.
  Please join me in supporting the GMC 2025 Trail Day on September 20, 2025. It will be cooler by
then!                                                                               Debbie Marcus

INGE BROWN (3/3/1934 – 8/1/2025)
                                     A very personal remembering.

                                                                  Inge Brown left us – on her own
                                                                terms – the way she lived.
                                                                I happened to visit her the day she
                                                                started voluntary cessation of eating
                                                                and drinking (VCED) to end her life
                                                                under Hospice care. "I had a good life"
                                                                she said, "but the more interesting part
                                                                was before I joined the O-Section".
                                                                Well, I did not know her then.
                                                                All I remember are the last thirty years
                                                                with Inge in the Green Mountain Club.
                                                                She came into our lives in December
                                                                1995 by crashing the Trebitz' New
                                                                Year's Eve Party, showing up
                                                                unannounced as a friend of the
                                                                Janeways. During the following years
                                                                she made up for it by offering countless
                                                                dinners, celebrations, parties and
                                                                invitations at her house, always with a
                                                                spread of lots of delicious food,
                                                                beautifully arranged. Inge was an
                                                                excellent cook and loved to entertain.
                                                                With her wide-ranging interests in
                                                                opera, music, ballroom dancing, foreign
                                                                countries, travel, crop lines, Osher
                                                                classes, - friends and acquaintances
                                                                from all over the world enjoyed her
                                                                hospitality. On the home ground, she
                                                                joined and led bike rides and short
                                                                hikes, often coming up with new trails
or venues, and helped on workdays. From 2008 to 2015 she was President of our O-Section.
  With the troubles of getting older, Inge had to give up some of her beloved activities, like downhill
skiing, dancing, sailing, even biking, but she never complained. She stayed her independent feisty self,
always conscious of her appearance, sometimes a bit bossy (I called her the General) but always
generous, cheerful and optimistic.                                                   Inge Trebitz

  Inge Brown had often mentioned to her friends that she did not want a memorial planned after her death.
Instead she would make sure a big happy party could be for arranged later.
The timing and place for Inge's party is not going to be known for a while. In the meantime we in the O-Section
should think about an informal gathering or potluck in her memory.

                           AN INVITATION TO JOIN THE GREEN MOUNTAIN CLUB
Who are we? Founded in 1910, the Green Mountain Club was established "to make the Vermont mountains play larger part in the
lives of people". To meet that goal, the club constructed the 272-mile Long Trail (LT). The GMC is responsible for developing
policies and programs aimed at preservation, maintenance, and proper use of hiking trails for the benefit of the people of Vermont.

What is the Upper Valley Ottauquechee Section? Our section, one of 14 chapters of the GMC, is located in and around the Upper
Valley. We schedule activities all year round for those who enjoy the outdoors, including hikes, bike trips, canoe outings, cross
country skiing and snow shoe hikes. The "UVO" Section is reponsible for the maintenance of 46 miles of the Appalachian Trail (AT)
between Maine Junction at Willard Pass near Killington and the Connecticut River at Norwich, VT, including four trail shelters.

We invite you to join! If you wish to participate in our activities, meet friendly, outdoors loving people and contribute to the
maintenance and quality of Vermont's hiking trail system, the Upper Valley Ottauquechee Section welcomes you.. Through the
quarterly GMC Long Trail News and the "UVO"Section Footnotes you will get information about section and main club activities
and issues of interest. For more information, call Iris Berezin at 802-295-2294.
