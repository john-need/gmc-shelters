                                                JANE CURTIS, one of the 23 founding members of the Ottauquechee
                                                Section, died on Feb. 7, at the age of 103. Jane and her husband Will were
                                                among the almost 20 hikers on the first two trips of the O-Section, covering
                                                the entire 18 miles of AT for which the section would be responsible, from
                                                LT junction to Rt. 12.
                                                   Jane was the O’s first section delegate to the GMC and section president
                                                from 1988 to 1991. This was a time when most of our portion of the AT
                                                was relocated to remove it from old roads and turn it into an actual trail.
                                                The O’s members responded to a letter from Jane and quickly raised the
                                                needed $1000 for the Long Trail Protection Fund.
                                                Jane and Will led many hiking and XC ski trips, (often close to their
                                                Hartland home, now Cobb Hill CoHousing), in addition to canoe outings,
                                                sometimes overnight trips, including a hiking vacation in England. Also a
                                                painter, Jane sometimes brought her watercolors on trips, and would
                                                produce beautiful paintings of the surroundings. Jane and Will also gave
                                                entertaining talks with photos from their adventures.
                                                Jane led a long and active life. She served on the GMC board, and was
                                                involved in many other organizations. She and Will ran The Yankee
                                                Bookshop in Woodstock, and traveled all over the world, often to remote
                                                locations. They authored six books including “Green Mountain Adventure,
                                                Vermont’s Long Trail”, for the GMC’s 75th anniversary, and “The Nature
                                                of Things”, a collection of Will’s well-known short radio programs. Jane
                                                was a truly amazing woman, and the club was fortunate to benefit from her
                                                involvement.                                                  Debbie Hatch

Trail Checking Conundrum
                                          Feb.1, Podunk Pond, S. Strafford.

                                        Bluebird sky, plenty of fluffy snow, good
                                        company – what else would we need for a
                                        great back-country ski trip? The leaders had
                                        checked the first mile of the trail and the
                                        last one, both in fine shape. The stretch in-
                                        between? It was in good condition last
                                        summer.
                                        Well, things changed as soon as we passed
                                        the wire fence and sign for Podunk Wildlife
                                        Management. Lots of fallen trees made for
                                        intricate ski maneuvers over, under,
                                        through and around. Taking skis off made
                                        you sink in and cause trouble putting them
                                        on again.
                                        But it was a fun trip anyway, and if the
                                        leaders had checked that section
                                        beforehand they might have canceled the

UVO Section First Aid                       Story by Bob Hagen
Nine trip leaders and candidates for chainsaw certification
attended a first aid class taught by fourth year medical
student, Joe Minichiello. Joe slanted the course towards
wilderness first aid, covering everything from tourniquets to
using a tube of cake frosting to revive a hiker who had
bonked due to low blood sugar. The attendees were: Dick
Andrews, Kathy Astrauckas and Bob Hagen, John
Dettwiler, Tom Kahl, Nick Krembs, Tom McCleary and
Heinz and Inge Trebitz.
The Shelters and Trails Committee has adopted the slogan
“Each one Teach one”, so if you’re on a hike with one of
these people, ask them to talk about their takeaways from
the course. Upper Valley Ambulance Service holds classes on a regular basis. If there’s interest we can set up
another class. Cost is $35.00.                                                                    Page - 5 -

                           AN INVITATION TO JOIN THE GREEN MOUNTAIN CLUB
Who are we? Founded in 1910, the Green Mountain Club was established "to make the Vermont mountains play a larger part in the
lives of people". To meet that goal, the club constructed the 272-mile Long Trail (LT). The GMC is responsible for developing
policies and programs aimed at preservation, maintenance, and proper use of hiking trails for the benefit of the people of Vermont.

What is the Upper Valley Ottauquechee Section? Our section, one of 14 chapters of the GMC, is located in and around the Upper
Valley. We schedule activities all year round for those who enjoy the outdoors, including hikes, bike trips, canoe outings, cross
country skiing and snow shoe hikes. The "UVO" Section is responsible for the maintenance of 46 miles of Appalachian Trail (AT)
between Maine Junction at Willard Pass near Killington and the Connecticut River at Norwich, VT, including four trail shelters.

We invite you to join! If you wish to participate in our activities, meet friendly, outdoors loving people and contribute to the
maintenance and quality of Vermont's hiking trail system, the Upper Valley Ottauquechee Section welcomes you. Through the
quarterly GMC Long Trail News and the "UVO" Section Footnotes you will get information about section and main club activities
and issues of interest. For more information, call Iris Berezin at 802-295-2294.
For membership, fill out the application below and send to Green Mountain Club, 4711 Waterbury-Stowe Road,
Waterbury Center, VT 05677, att. Rick Dugan/Membership. Please enclose a check, payable to the Green Mountain Club.
