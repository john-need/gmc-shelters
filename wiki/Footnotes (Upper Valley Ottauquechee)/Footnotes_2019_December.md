COMMUNICATION ISSUES — WHEN AND WHERE SHOULD WE Yield?

   At our Oct.26, 2019, Annual Meeting the question was brought up if the name change to “Upper Valley" O-Section made
   us more visible or recognizable within our local community and beyond. While we could not pinpoint any clear results,
“we discussed again how to improve the way we communicate and advertise what activities our section is involved with.

   According to the Oct. 2019 membership list, we have 305 members, 88 of whom are listed with a partner. This means that
                                                         the three-monthly newsletter and the outings schedule gets sent to
                                                         217 addresses, either by email (47) or by postal mail (170).
                                                          Besides the official newsletter, Heinz Trebitz maintains an
                                                         unofficial and optional email list, used to make the participants
                                                         aware of trip changes or spur-of-the-moment planned activities.

                                                              But what if members of other GMC sections or of the general
                                                              public would like to find out about our trips and possibly
                                                              participate? How do they get the information?

                                                              They could go to the GMC website, find the individual sections, click on Upper
                                                              Valley Ottauquechee Section and then on FOOTNOTES (newsletter), which
                                                              includes the schedule for three months. Peter Hope always transfers the newest
                                                              edition onto the website, as soon as the newsletter editor sends it to him.

                                                              However, this system does not work if someone just wants to know
                                                              which activities are planned for a certain date anywhere within the
                                                              GMC community. You would have to go to the “GMC Calendar of
                                                              Outings and Events”. This google calendar features squares for
                                                              each day of the month. Within the square, a section’s outing shows
                                                              up with a heading on a color-coded bar. The O-Section is
                                                              represented with a brown bar. For Nov.2, 2019, for example, it
                                                              said "Elmore Mtn.". If you clicked on it, the description of the trip
                                                              appeared similar to what it lookes in our newsletter.

                                                              Good idea, but: Right now, this description has to be typed in for
                                                              each event, because our schedule is put together in a “Word”
                                                              format; it could not be automatically transferred to a Google
        ;                                   x                 calendar. We are working hard to find a solution to bridge this gap
   and eliminate the tedious work of individually typing in each trip. The change may take some time, and it may involve
   switching the format of our O-Section schedule to “Google Excel” in the near future. STAY TUNED!                           Inge Trebitz




   "RUN! RUN! RUN!"
   Little Reuben called as soon as he was released from his mother's
   carrier. On Nov.2, Susan Ogden's grandson and daughter Hannah
   had joined a group of O-Section hikers on a beautiful fall day loop
   to the fire tower of Elmore Mountain. First a wide and gently
   climbing CCC road, then a twisting and somewhat wet and
   slippery trail up to the ruins of the warden's cabin. Finally a steep
   ascent to the gorgeous view of the Worcester Range and beyond
   from the tower.
   A somewhat easier and longer trail took us back down. By the
   time we reached the wide road again, we could hardly keep up
   with Reuben's running energy!



   We wish we had more young people to join
   our trips and fire us up!                             Inge Trebitz

                            AN INVITATION TO JOIN THE GREEN MOUNTAIN CLUB
Who are we? Founded in 1910, the Green Mountain Club was established "to make the Vermont mountains play a larger part in the
lives of people". To meet that goal, the club constructed the 272-mile Long Trail (LT). The GMC is responsible for developing
policies and programs aimed at preservation, maintenance, and proper use of hiking trails for the benefit of the people of Vermont.

What is the Upper Valley Ottauquechee Section? Our section, one of 14 chapters of the GMC, is located in and around the Upper
Valley. We schedule activities all year round for those who enjoy the outdoors, including hikes, bike trips, canoe outings, cross
country skiing and snow shoe hikes. The "O" Section is responsible for the maintenance of 46 miles of Appalachian Trail (AT)
between Maine Junction at Willard Pass near Killington and the Connecticut River at Norwich, VT, including four trail shelters.

We invite you to join! If you wish to participate in our activities, meet friendly, outdoors loving people and contribute to the
maintenance and quality of Vermont's hiking trail system, the Upper Valley Ottauquechee Section welcomes you. Through the
quarterly GMC Long Trail News and the "O"Section Footnotes you will get information about section and main club activities and
issues of interest. For more information, call Iris Berezin at 802-295-2294.
