                                        SUSAN BAZAAR RE,MEMBERED

WELGOME TO OUR NEW MEMBERS                     Sue Bazaar, an O-Section member since
                                        1988, died onFebruary 16 at the age of68. For
Sheila Kaplow             Kate Nadler   nine years she had suffered from a lung disease
                                        that gradually sapped her strength. When I
Chloe Ritter              Ted Jabbs
                                        visited her at the hospital three days before her
Marc Lefebvre & Elizabeth Pappius       death, she said 'oI am done fighting".
Tim & Janet Taylor Jill & Jeff Thomas           Sue was my (and RickEthier's) frequent
         Charles & JiIt Castelli        hiking companion in the nineties, when all three
                                        of us worked on completing the Long Trail in
                                        mostly week-end pieces. Sue was a strong, always
                                        cheerful hiking partner, never complaining. But
                                        what I admired most was her graceful hiking
Sue Bazaar                              style. She seemed to skip along the trail like a
On the AT                               mountain goat, finding the easiest way through
In Maine                                the mud and over rocks and roots. When I
                                        mentioned this to her during my hospital visit,
                                        she smiled: "No wonder! When I came home
                                        from work and the kids from school, we went
                                        into the woods below Ascutney almost every day.
                                        That's the way to learn!"
                                                                              Inge Trebitz
