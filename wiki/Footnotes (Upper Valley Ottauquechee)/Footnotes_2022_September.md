On and Off the AT:
             Trail Angels and a Wedding

    On July 18 I checked my segment of the AT at the
Rte. 12 crossing. That place, you know, where Japanese
Knotweed is out of control, forming a 50' tunnel to
duck through. Eventually we'll have to install some
lights to find the way.
Surprise: A trail angel had set up a station just as you
come off the bridge. Soda, crackers, grilled hot dogs –
you name it. Her name? Pamela Crigler, spending the
summer months in Woodstock. Not a club member –
just an Angel. I thanked her, and we spent some time
chatting; there were Nobos and Sobos passing through
or taking a break. One guy was from Holland.
    Talking about foreign visitors on the trail:
Bob Hagen acted as Justice of the Peace in the wedding of a German couple thru-hiking the AT. Laura and
Lukas had started hiking the AT three years ago. This year they came to finish the hike (Katahdin on August 8),
but wanted to do so as a married couple. On June 24 Bob officiated the ceremony on Houghton Hill in Thetford.
Kathy Astrauckas (who knew the two from the trail) and I had the privilege to do the photos.
We wish Laura and Lukas many years of hiking together and taking the hills in stride!            Heinz Trebitz

                           AN INVITATION TO JOIN THE GREEN MOUNTAIN CLUB
Who are we? Founded in 1910, the Green Mountain Club was established "to make the Vermont mountains play a larger part in the
lives of people". To meet that goal, the club constructed the 272-mile Long Trail (LT). The GMC is responsible for developing
policies and programs aimed at preservation, maintenance, and proper use of hiking trails for the benefit of the people of Vermont.

What is the Upper Valley Ottauquechee Section? Our section, one of 14 chapters of the GMC, is located in and around the Upper
Valley. We schedule activities all year round for those who enjoy the outdoors, including hikes, bike trips, canoe outings, cross
country skiing and snow shoe hikes. The "UVO" Section is responsible for the maintenance of 46 miles of Appalachian Trail (AT)
between Maine Junction at Willard Pass near Killington and the Connecticut River at Norwich, VT, including four trail shelters.

We invite you to join! If you wish to participate in our activities, meet friendly, outdoors loving people and contribute to the
maintenance and quality of Vermont's hiking trail system, the Upper Valley Ottauquechee Section welcomes you. Through the
quarterly GMC Long Trail News and the "UVO" Section Footnotes you will get information about section and main club activities
and issues of interest. For more information, call Iris Berezin at 802-295-2294.
For membership, fill out the application below and send to Green Mountain Club, 4711 Waterbury-Stowe Road,
Waterbury Center, VT 05677, att. Rick Dugan/Membership. Please enclose a check, payable to the Green Mountain Club.
