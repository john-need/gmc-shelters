                                                  Camp	
  News	
  
                                                       	
  
The	
  GMC,	
  Worcester	
  Section,	
  camp	
  is	
  located	
  at	
  345	
  Rawson	
  Street	
  in	
  Leicester.	
  
It	
  is	
  open	
  from	
  dawn	
  to	
  dusk	
  for	
  picnicking,	
  boating,	
  swimming	
  and	
  fishing.	
  There	
  
is	
  no	
  overnight	
  camping	
  allowed.	
  There	
  is	
  no	
  lifeguard	
  on	
  duty,	
  so	
  swimming	
  is	
  at	
  
your	
  own	
  risk.	
  Please	
  be	
  watchful	
  of	
  your	
  children,	
  especially	
  while	
  they	
  are	
  in	
  
the	
  water.	
  Please	
  be	
  courteous	
  to	
  others	
  and	
  pack	
  out	
  your	
  own	
  trash.	
  	
  We	
  will	
  
continue	
  to	
  practice	
  social	
  distancing	
  and	
  wear	
  facemasks	
  when	
  in	
  contact	
  with	
  
others.	
  	
  
                                                                  	
  
Please	
  secure	
  the	
  gate	
  when	
  you	
  enter	
  and	
  leave	
  the	
  camp.	
  The	
  camp	
  is	
  open	
  to	
  
the	
  public	
  by	
  foot	
  traffic	
  only	
  when	
  the	
  gate	
  is	
  locked.	
  	
  
	
  
The	
  camp	
  rules	
  are	
  located	
  in	
  a	
  green	
  box	
  on	
  a	
  tree	
  outside	
  the	
  camp	
  lodge.	
  
Please	
  take	
  time	
  to	
  read	
  them.	
  Thank	
  you	
  for	
  your	
  cooperation	
  in	
  keeping	
  our	
  
camp	
  safe	
  for	
  everyone.
