             Hiking
Summer
             Saturday, August 5. MidState Trail Hike. It’s called “Around Muddy Pond.” We
Activities   will be hiking from Ashburnham to Westminster. The distance is 5.7 miles. Please
             email Betty Jenewin at bettyjenewin@charter.net to sign up, or call Margos
             Stone at 508-799-0842 to register.

             Sunday, August 27. Sholan Point Sunset Hike - Wachusett Reservoir
             This approximately 5-mile round trip hike along old roads on DCR watershed land
             will loop near the Lily Ponds, then over French Hill through mature white pine
             and oak forest to Sholan Point on a remote shore of Wachusett Reservoir. Here
             you can get million-dollar views of the Reservoir looking west, south and east.
             The hike is timed to watch the sunset at the point. Bring mosquito repellent and
             a headlamp or flashlight for the hike back out. Contact Mike Peckar at 508-835-
             2959 to sign up.

             Saturday, September 9. Midstate Trail Hike. Hike through cow pastures at
             Maple Heights Farm, then by Round Meadow Pond and Wachusett Village Inn,
             then along Wyman Brook, total of almost 6 miles. Please email Betty Jenewin at
             bettyjenewin@charter.net to sign up, or call Margos Stone at 508-799-0842 to
             register.

Saturday, September 30. Hike the Pisgah Mountain Trail Loop at Pisgah State
Park, 520 Old Chesterfield Road, Winchester, New Hampshire. Pisgah State Park
includes over 13,300 acres of rough forested terrain, encompassing a complete
watershed north of the Ashuelot River. Within the Cheshire County towns of
Winchester, Chesterfield and Hinsdale, the park protects seven ponds (popular
for fishing), four highland ridges and numerous wetlands. The area’s 21 square
miles make it the largest property in the New Hampshire state park system. The
total hiking distance of the Pisgah Mountain Trail Loop is 8.5 miles. Please call
Margos Stone at 508-799-0842 to register and for meeting time and place.
Carpooling is available from Worcester. You may also email Margos at
mstone12242@ charter.net. Richard Lombard is co-leader of this hike. You can
reach him at rllombard2@gmail.com or call him at 978-394-9619.
