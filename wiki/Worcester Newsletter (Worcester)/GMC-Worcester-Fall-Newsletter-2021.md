President’s Message Fall 2021
Dear Members,

Please join us for the fall activities listed in this newsletter. These activities
include a hike, mountain bike ride, a horseshoe tournament, a camp clean up,
trail work in Vermont and the Worcester Section annual meeting. Of particular
note will be our annual meeting on Saturday, October 30, at the camp. This will
include a ham and bean picnic dinner prepared by our own great chefs, followed
by a speaker to be announced. We hope that you will attend. Please see the
activities schedule for details. Activities can also be found on our website by
Googling Green Mountain Club Worcester.

Thank you for your support and membership in the Green Mountain Club,
Worcester Section. If you have any questions, suggestions or concerns about the
GMC, please feel free to reach out to me, or any of our other officers.

Best regards,

Ram Moennsad, President

Saturday, September 18. Mountain Bike Ride over the backcountry roads of the
Ware River Watershed. We will meet at 9:30 am at the trailhead of the Midstate
Trail parking lot in Rutland on Route 122. Coming from Worcester, the parking lot
will be on your right. The ride will be approximately 10 miles over moderate
terrain. Please preregister for the ride by calling Dick Kisten at 508-892-8866
after 7:00 pm or leave a message, or call Margos Stone at 508-826-6842 or email
him at mstone12242@charter.net with any questions.

Saturday, September 25. Hike the trails of the Cut Throat Brook Tree Farm in
Athol. We will meet at 9:30 am at the Tree Farm, which is located on 586 Briggs
Road in Athol on the Athol Petersham line. This will be a 4 -mile hike over
moderate terrain, followed be an optional picnic at a nearby horse farm. Please
call Gary Brigham at 508-471-7867 or email him at gbrighammt@aol.com with
any questions or to preregister for the hike. Please bring a lunch for the picnic if
you plan to attend the picnic. Directions: Take Route 122 North from Worcester
to 32 North in Petersham. Follow 32 North for approximately 5 miles to Briggs
Road at the Athol Petersham line. Turn right on Briggs Road and drive 1/3rd mile
to the parking lot on the left, or use Google Maps for 586 Briggs Road in Athol.

Sunday, October 10 at noon. Single Elimination Horseshoe Tournament at the
GMC camp, 345 Rawson Street in Leicester. The tournament will be from noon to
2:00 pm, followed by a potluck picnic. Folks can come to play, or to watch and
cheer on the players. The cost to play in the tournament is $5 per person. The
prize will be $50 for the winner. The balance of the proceeds will be donated to
the GMC, Worcester Section, as a fundraiser. Please bring a lunch if you plan to
attend the potluck picnic. If you wish to preregister or have any questions, please
email Pat Howe at phowe@wpi.edu.

Saturday and Sunday, October 16 and 17. Join us for the Worcester Section Fall
Work Party on the Stratton Mountain Section of the Long Trail. Some of us will
car pool from Paxton at 7:00 a.m. Some of us will meet at 9:30 a.m. on Saturday,
October 16 at the LT/AT Trailhead on the Arlington West-Wardsboro Rd. (Kelly
Stand Rd). For driving directions, Google 1376 Stratton Arlington Road, Stratton,
Vermont. Call Steve Crowe at 508-450-1091 for more information, or email him
at steve@sunshinelandscapingco.com.

Saturday, October 23, Camp Clean Up. We will meet at 9:30 am at the camp for
a clean up of the grounds, lodge and change house. Bring rakes, shovels, brush
cutters, chain saws and cleaning supplies. Coffee and donuts will be provided.
Call Ram with any questions at 508-210-6965 or email him at
shivratri@gmail.com. We hope you can join us.
