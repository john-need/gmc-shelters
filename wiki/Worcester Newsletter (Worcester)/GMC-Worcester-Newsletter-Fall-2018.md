                         President’s Message
Fall | 2018
                         Dear Members,
_____________
                         Please join us for some of our fall activities listed in this newsletter. These
___________________
                         include hikes, canoeing, picnics at the camp, bicycling and many other events.
                        Of particular note will be our annual meeting on Saturday, November 17, at the
                        camp. This will include a ham and bean supper prepared by our own great chefs,
                        and a slide show of a recent nine-day backpacking trip in the Cairngorms
                        National Park in Scotland by David Miller, one of our own members. Please see
                        the activities schedule for details.

                        Please join us for our Potluck Picnic at the camp on Saturday, October 20 at
                        12:00 noon. See the activity schedule for details.

                        The outdoor bathroom is in dire shape and we may have to replace the entire
                        unit next year.

                        The bridge construction on Rawson Street should be finished in early November
                        so that we can all visit the camp more frequently.

                        I am requesting all members who can assist in any way to help maintain our
                        treasured camp. Your active participation as well as your ideas will make the
                        camp better and more enjoyable. You can always call or write to me with your
                        views to update and improve the camp. I look forward to seeing you at the
                        annual meeting. As always, it is your camp and it is a few who aspire to keep it
                        going. Be one of them.

                        Best regards,

                        Ram Moennsad, President

             Saturday, October 6. Camp clean up at 345 Rawson Street in Leicester, 9:00
   Fall      a.m. to noon. We will work on cleaning up the grounds and the lodge. Bring
             rakes, shovels, a wheel barrel, pruning shears and cleaning supplies. We will
             repair erosion damage to the grounds caused by recent heavy rains. Coffee and
Activities   donuts will be supplied. Call Ram at 508-210-6965 or Margos at 508-826-6842
             for more information.

             Saturday, October 13, paddle in the land of King Phillip. We will paddle the
             Great Swamp in Rhode Island and enjoy the fall colors. We will encounter beaver
             dams and may encounter fallen trees obstructing the waterway. Considering
             recent subnormal rains, we may encounter shallow water shoals where we will
             be forced to wade and drag our watercraft. Call leader David Miller at 508-853-
             8964 to register and for meeting time and place.

             Saturday and Sunday, October 13 and 14. Join us for the Worcester Section Fall
             Work Party on the Stratton Mountain Section of the Long Trail. Some of us will
             car pool from Paxton at 7:00 a.m. Some of us will meet at 9:30 a.m. on Saturday,
             October 13 at the LT/AT Trailhead on the Arlington West-Wardsboro Rd. (Kelly
             Stand Rd). For driving directions, Google 1376 Stratton Arlington Road, Stratton,
             Vermont. Call Steve Crowe at 508-892-9942 for more information, or email him
             at steve@sunshinelandscapingco.com.

             Saturday, October 20, 12:00 noon. Potluck Picnic at the GMC camp in Leicester.
             Bring your favorite dish to share. Hot dogs, hamburgers, beans, coffee and
             beverages will be provided. We will have a short hike after the luncheon for
             those interested. For more information, call or text Ram Moennsad at 508-210-
             6965 or email him at shivratri@gmail.com. Remember the Rawson Street bridge
             may be still out if you are coming from Spencer.

             Saturday, October 27, Bicycle ride on the back-country roads of the Ware River
             Watershed. Trip will be about 5-8 miles. Meet at the parking lot on Route 122 in
             Rutland at the trailhead to the Mid State Trail. Call or text leader Ram Moennsad
             at 508-210-6965 or Dick Kisten at 508-892-8866 to register and for meeting time.

             Saturday, November 3, Canoe Day Trip on the Ware River from Barre to
             Gilbertville, passing historic King Phillip’s camp and portaging around a mill dam.
             Considering recent subnormal rains, we may encounter shallow water shoals
             where we will be forced to wade and drag our watercraft. Call leader David
             Miller at 508-853-8964.

             Saturday, November 10. Hike the Pisgah Mountain Trail Loop at Pisgah State
             Park, 520 Old Chesterfield Road, Winchester, New Hampshire. The total hiking
             distance of the Pisgah Mountain Trail Loop is 8.5 miles. To register Margos Stone
             at 508-826-6842 or mstone12242@ charter.net. Carpooling is available from
             Worcester. You can also contact co-leader Richard Lombard at 978-394-9619 or
             rllombard2@gmail.com for details about meeting time and place.

Saturday, November 17. GMC, Worcester Section, Annual Meeting. Social Hour
from 4 to 5 pm, Baked Ham Dinner at 5:00 pm, Business Meeting at 6:00 pm,
followed by our guest speaker and member, David Miller, who will speak and
show slides about our recent backpacking trip at the Cairngorms National Park in
Scotland, United Kingdom. To register for the meeting, send in the reservation
form attached to your newsletter to Margos Stone, Treasurer, 1A Kensington
Heights, Worcester, MA 01602. Or you may make your reservations for the
meeting by calling or texting Margos at 508-826-6842 or emailing him at
mstone12242@charter.net. The cost is $10.00 per person for the dinner and can
be paid at the door.

Walk off the Bird at Trout Brook Reservation in Holden, MA, Saturday morning,
November 24. Burn off those tryptophan-induced extra calories on this 5-mile
loop over the White Oak, Bob Elms, and Red trails at Trout Brook. If there is
snow or ice, bring microspikes or snowshoes, depending on the depth, and the
route will be adjusted. Contact Mike Peckar at mike@gmcwoo.org or 508-835-
2959 for the exact time and meeting location.

Saturday, December 1, Canoe trip on the Assabet River. With the leaves gone,
the rivers will be up again; so, we will do the Assabet River from Northborough
to Hudson. Remember, winter doesn’t begin until December 21. Considering
recent sub normal rains, we may encounter shallow water shoals where we will
be forced to wade and drag our watercraft. Call leader David Miller at (508) 853-
8964 to register.

Sunday, December 16. Hike the trails of the Federated Women’s Club State
Forest, off Route 122 in Petersham. Enjoy views of the Quabbin Resevoir. Call
Margos Stone at 508-799-0842 or Dick Kisten at 508-892-8866 after 7:00 p.m. for
meeting time and place.

Sunday, December 30. Hike or snow shoe the trails of Wachusett Meadow
Wildlife Sancturary, 113 Goodnow Road in Princeton. We will meet in the
parking lot at Wachusett Meadows at 9:30 a.m. The hike will be approximately 4
miles over moderate terrain. Call leader Margos Stone at 508-799-0842 or email
him at mstone12242 @charter.net to register.

January 1, 2019, 12:00 noon. President’s Open House at the camp. Enjoy a pot
luck luncheon by the wood stove. Bring your favorite dish to share. Coffee, cider
and dessert will be provided. Call Ram at 508-210-6965 or Margos at 508-799-
0842 for more information or to register. A short hike will follow the luncheon
for those interested.

                    345 Rawson Street, Leicester, MA
Beginning in July the gate to the camp at 345 Rawson Street in Leicester has
been locked. Please see your email or print version of this newsletter for the
code.

If you have a problem unlocking the gate, please call Margos at 508-826-6842 or
Ram at 508-210-6965. In the future, the code to the lock may change, but we will
inform you first in the newsletter. Stay tuned to our quarterly newsletter.



                   Receiving Your Newsletter by Email
If you are receiving this newsletter by regular mail and would like to receive it by
email, please email Margos at mstone12242@charter.net. Give us your
preferred email address and you will receive future newsletters by email. This
will save the club on mailing costs and make it more convenient for you. Thank
you.



                  Contributions to the GMC, Worcester Section

Your contributions to the GMC, Worcester Section, are welcome and needed.
Our income from dues alone is not sufficient to meet the expenses for the GMC,
Worcester Section. Your tax-deductible contribution will help us to improve and
maintain our camp on Cedar Meadow Lake. We depend on contributions to
balance our budget. You can mail your contribution to Margos Stone, Treasurer,
GMC Worcester Section, 1A Kensington Heights, Worcester, MA 01602. All
donations are tax-deductible. Donations can also be made on our website at
http://www.gmcwoo.org.
