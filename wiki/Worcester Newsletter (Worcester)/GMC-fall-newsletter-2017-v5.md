I will focus on our work achievement during the last month.

   1. Gerry Vayo and I have dismantled the dry wood shed because it was
      falling apart. We have taken down almost all of it. The roofing shingles
      were removed and were sent for recycling at the Spencer recycling
      center. We had a total of 3 drums of the shingles plus the particle board
      debris. There is more to be done and we anticipate completing the
      demolition project by winter. There are no future plans to rebuild
      another one and we will store the dry wood in the shed.
   2. A truck load of stones was delivered and we have already stabilized the
      fireplace and picnic are with ¾ in stones. Dick Kisten assisted us
      considerably in this task. Some of the large stone sticking out from the
      ground were jack hammered by Gerry Vayo and myself. We still need to
      move the gravel to sloping zones and slippery areas. We could use all the
      assistance of members for this. Please feel free to contact the camp
      committee if you would like to make muscles lifting and moving the
      gravel.
   3. We will be starting to cut some saplings from overcrowding after the fall
      when all the trees have shed their leaves.
   4. Our portable toilet is falling apart and it is a matter of replacing it with a
      new one with a new foundation or build a new one which will be
      biodegradable. I am an advocate of a biodegradable one which will be
      roomy also. There is one at Sibley farm installed by GWLT (Greater
      Worcester Land Trust). We are also looking at the economical way of
      replacing the toilet. Any feedback from our members is welcome.
   5. Margos Stone and I had a meeting with the Town of Leicester Assessor
      regarding having the camp open as well maintaining the nonprofit status.
      It was suggested that we have a gate with a lock accessed by members
      only. It could have a sign stating, “Open from sunrise to sunset” and
      another sign saying, “foot traffic.” This is acceptable to the Assessor.
      Please let me know if any of our Worcester section members have
      expertise in manufacturing a metal gate or know of someone who could
      assist us.
   6. Mike Peckar has taken the responsibilities for a web page for our section
      and it is under construction. It will be linked to our main headquarters’
      web site. We hope that our quarterly newsletter will be published on the
      web page gmcwoo.org

Finally, I encourage all to participate in our activities and camp projects. I am
always seeking your feedback and your suggestions to improve our camp
facilities for all to enjoy.


Best regards,

Ram Moennsad, President

Sunday, November 19, Federated Women’s Club State Forest. Hike the trails of
off Route 122 in Petersham. Enjoy views of the Quabbin Reservoir. Call Margos
Stone at 508-799-0842 or Dick Kisten at 508-892-8866 after 7:00 p.m. to register.

Saturday, November 25, 9:30 A.M. Hike: Walk Off the Bird.
This hike will explore the open fields and woodlands or Rauscher Farm that was
the last working farm in Clinton. The farm, which was saved from development
in 2006 by the town is now an open space reserve. The hike will be
approximately 4 miles and make a loop through DCR property around Clamshell
Pond. Optionally, following the hike, we will explore the 1000’ long abandoned
tunnel of the Mass. Central Railroad. Registration is required. Contact Mike
Peckar at mike@wachusettgreenways.com or call 508-835-2959.

Saturday, December 10. Hike the Pisgah Mountain Trail Loop at Pisgah State
Park, 520 Old Chesterfield Road, Winchester, New Hampshire. Pisgah State Park
includes over 13,300 acres of rough forested terrain, encompassing a complete
watershed north of the Ashuelot River. The total hiking distance of the Pisgah
Mountain Trail Loop is 8.5 miles. Please call Margos Stone at 508-799-0842 to
register and for meeting time and place. Carpooling is available from Worcester.
You may also email Margos at mstone12242@ charter.net. Richard Lombard is
co-leader of this hike. You can reach him at rllombard2@gmail.com or call him at
978-394-9619.

Sunday, December 17, 9:30 am. Hike or cross country ski depending on
conditions on the Mass Central Rail Trail, Mid State Trail and on country roads
within the watershed. Meet at the parking lot on Route 122 at the trailhead to
the Mid State Trail in Rutland. Call Dick Kisten at 508-892-8866 after 7 pm to
register and for more information.
