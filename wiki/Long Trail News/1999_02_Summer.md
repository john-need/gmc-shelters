   *DIFFICULTY RATING
   FOR CHIWREN'S HIKES

   EASY: fewer than 2 miles round
   trip; fewer than 500 feet elevation
   gain; appropriate for kids ages three
   and up.
   MODERATE: 2 to 4 miles round
   trip; 500 to 1,500 feet elevation gain;
   rough or uneven trail; appropriate
   for kids ages five and up.
   CHALLENGING: more than 4 miles
   round trip; more than 1,500 feet
   elevation gain; rough or uneven
   trail; appropriate for kids ages seven
   and up.
   These ratings provide a general
   guideline. The difficulty of a hike
   depends on each child's ability.
