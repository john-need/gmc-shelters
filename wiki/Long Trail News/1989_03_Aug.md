   On April 21 the long anticipated pre-
scribed burn of Spring Lake Clearing was
completed. Spring Lake Clearing, located on
the Long Trail between Route 140 and Route
103, was a former pasture slowly being
reclaimed by forest. Identified as a prime site
for a prescribed burn during a 1987 open-
areas management inventory, prep work was
begun in 1988 with an eye towards a burn in
the spring of 1989. (See February Long Trail
News) .
   The past winters light snow fall in southern
Vermont promised an early burn season. By
late March, plans were being formulated for
an early April burn. A group of fifteen poten-
tial volunteers were contacted and the waiting
game began. Prescribed burns are dependent
on many weather factors including wind,
humidity, and lack of rain. A minimum of two
to three days of sunshine are required to dry
the burn site. Any one who has spent time
in Vermont during the early spring knows
how rarely this occurs. This spring proved no
different.

day of sunshine appeared April 21. Early

                                                   Profile of the Artist
An artist since the age of 5, Sue Westin has exhibited her wildlife art in major exhibitions across the United States. She
works primarily in watercolors and oils and her works are in numerous private collections. Besides wildlife; her subjects
include landscapes, seascapes, still lifes and people. Sue has been a northern Vermont resident since 1975 . She was one
of Vermont's first State Park Naturalists and as such spent several summers at Button Bay State Park on the shores of
Lake Champlain. Raised in Connecticut, she has also summered on Cape Cod where she developed an interest in shore birds.

Recent shows include:
* September-November, 1987, 1988 & 1989; the prestigious juried international show, "Birds in Art", Leigh Yawkey
  Woodson Art Museum, Wausau, WI
* September-October, 1980-88, juried annual show, "The Vermont Institute of Natural Science", Woodstock, VT
* 1979-88, juried national show, "Society of Animal Artists" annual exhibitions, which are held throughout the United States.
