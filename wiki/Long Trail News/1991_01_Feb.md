              GMC REACHES AGREEMENT WITH KILLINGTON/PICO
        On January 7, six months of negotiations
    between Vermont conservation groups and
    Killington and Pico ski areas concluded with
    the signing of a final agreement that address
    many environmental issue related to the
    merger and expansion of the two ski resorts.
    The talks that led to th agreement were
    mediated by Ira Lobel of rhe Federal Media-
    tion and Conciliation Service, and were sup-
    ported by the National Park Service, whiCh
    is conducting an environmental review of trail
    protection alternatives in the area.
       1n addition to the two ski areas, parties
    to the agreement included the Green Moun-
    tain Club, Appalachian Trail Conference,
    Connecticut River Watershed Council,
    Shrewsbury Land Trust, Vermont Audubon
    Oouncil, Vermont Group of the Sierra Club
    and Vermont Natural Resources Council.
    Th agreement was witnessed by Governor
    Madeleine Kunin and U .S. Senators Patrick
    Leahy andJamesjefTords, all of whom played
    important roles in bringing the negotiations
    about.
       As can be seen on the accompanying map,
    the agreement covers lands south of Pico Peak.
    It is a very long and complex document that
    deals with many issues: protection of the
    Long/ Appalachian Trail, wildlife habitat
    preservation, the physical linking of Killington
    and Pico limits on future growth of the ski
    areas, and a process to monitor th en-

  The Silver Lake area east of Lake Dunmore
was hike leader Eric Seidel's destination in late

     We departed from Montpelier under
  a frosty and beautifully clear morning
  sky. Some unusual hikers joined us. A
  young Australian woman on a bicycle
  trip to Montreal read about the hike
  while staying in a local youth hostel. She
  decided that she was ready for a 10 mile
  hike after cycling from White River
  Junction the day before. Kavita Noble
  brought her two month old daughter in
  a Snugli pack-mother was indomitable
  and the infant serene for the whole trip!
  We left the traiJhead about 10 a.m.,
  climbed up to Silver Lake (east of Lake
  Dunmore) and picked up the Chandler
  Ridge Trail which we followed south for
  about four miles with many wonderful
  views to the east and west. The return
  trip via Leicester Hollow appeared
  almost tropical with thick beds of lush
  ferns. The trail crossed and recrossed
  Leicester Hollow Brook many times.
  We arrived at the trailhead about 4 p. m.
  after a day of beautiful scenery, perfect
  weather, and wonderful company. All
  agreed that this loop will make a great
  ski trip. And, I am confident that baby
  Noble must be one of the youngest to
  do a hike of this length!
