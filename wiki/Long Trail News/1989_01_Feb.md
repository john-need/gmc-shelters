                                                                                                   Members of the Club will be sad-
                                                                                                dened to learn of the passing of Bob
                                                                                                Johnson on November 5, 1988 . Bob
                                                                                                and his wife Johanna had been regulars
                                                                                                at Intersectional for many years, always
                                                                                                traveling by bus from their home in
                                                                                                Somerville, Massachusetts. With their
                                                                                                son Johann , they completed the Long
                                                                                                Trail in the 1970's, later returning often
                                                                                                to his favorite stretch near Mt. Carmel.
                                                                                                Bob was well known for his walking
                                                                                                sticks cut one year, stripped, sanded and
                                                                                                varnished at home, and returned the
                                                                                                next year to become one of the
                                                                                                highlights of the Intersectional Auction.
                                                                                                He was also known for his pipe and
                                                                                                army fatigues, to which he had added
                                                                                                even more pockets. But perhaps Bob
                                                                                                will be best remembered for his beguil-
                                                                                                ing way with children and their obvious
                                                                                                affection for him. His strolls around the
                                                                                                Intersectional campground always in-
                                                                                                cluded a string of young admirers not
                                                                                                far behind. A retired Army veteran, he
                                                                                                was interred in the National Veteran's
