       "Summer time, and the living is easy ... " So
    go the words of the popular song, but the sum-
    mer of 1986 has been a time of nervousness
    and nail-biting as the Green Mountain Club
    has geared up for this major capital fund-
    raising campaign only to hear of new
    developments and more Long Trail lands go-
    ing up for sale.
       The initial wave of shock rippled down the
    Long Trail when several major forest land-
    owners announced, in the summer of 1985,
    that portions of their northern Vermont
    holdings were going on the market. A survey
    by GMC, financed by a grant from the Ellis
    L. Phillips Foundation, soon revealed how
    serious the problem was. As a result of a long
    slump in the timber products industry, half
    of the Trail on privately-owned land crossed

    maining owners indicated they would readi-
