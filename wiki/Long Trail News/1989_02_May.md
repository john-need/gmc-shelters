If you are interested in going on recrea-
tional trips and work parties sponsored by the
GMC you should consider joining a Section.
Your dues go directly to the Section (even if
you mail your dues to the office). The Club
collects an assesment of $5. 50 per member
from the Sections.
   If both reasons are important to you then
you can join as both a Section member and
an At-Large member. This way you can
financially support the work of the Green
Mountain Club, as well as participating in the
year round activities sponsored by the
Sections.
