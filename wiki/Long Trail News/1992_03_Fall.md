     The format changed very little over the
next fifteen years. In November 1940, the
 LIN became a small, sixteen page booklet. It
 returned to its original format in 1944. Then
in 1967, the masthead changed to include a
 map of Vennont.
     In 1985, changes were approved in
 format, as long as the publication didn't
become "too slick." Through the course of its
 evolution, we have kept to this idea. With the
 move to desktop publishing, we were able to
 modify the publication so that it was better
 organized, easier to read, and able to meet the
 needs of a growing GMC membership. The
 gradual changes that have occurred over the
 past seven years have culminated into the
 magazine which you see before you - a mix-
