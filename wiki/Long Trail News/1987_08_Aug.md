OTTAUQUECHEE SECTION/
 ALOHA WORK PROJECT
   On May 16 & 17th the GMC's Ottau-
quechee ("O") Section and the Appalachian
Long Distance Hikers Association (ALDHA)
worked together to open 1. 0 mile of rerouted
Appalachian Trail east of the Chatauguay
Road area.
   ALDHA has for several years held in-
formal work projects along the Appalachian
Trail system. This in response to the desire
of many distance hikers to get involved and
contribute something to the AT. Last spring
ALDHA decided that the time had come to
organized these somewhat sporadic work
hikes into a program of regular spring and fall
weekend projects. The program dubbed
"Trail-Aid", is to have a work trip occurring
in four regions of the AT on the same
weekend.
   GMC Field Supervisor, Ray Auger, and
ALDHA Work Trips Coordinator, Allen                  Side hill construction on AT reroute east of Chataguey Road. Jan Collins in foreground.

vania. Several members of the "O" Section
made arrangements for ALDHA members to
camp free at Silver Lake State Park and also
arranged for a Saturday evening pot luck
supper.
   A sunny, pleasant, black fly free weekend
greeted the five ALDHA, eight GMC and
three friends in attendance. On Saturday, this
group completed 0.2 mile of major sidehill ex-
cavation, laid out 0.1 mile of trail, brushed,
blazed and opened 1.0 mile of new AT. Ray
Auger was pleasantly surprised the sidehill ex-
cavation was completed in half a day as he
anticipated this to take much of the weekend.
   On Saturday evening the "O" Section
hosted an incredible feed. All the tired workers
certainly went to bed fat and happy! Sunday
a smaller crew "fine tuned" Saturdays work,
closing the old trail and officially opening the

TEMPORARY RELOCATION
   The Long Trail between Jonesville and
Wiley Lodge has been closed by the land-
owner. The temporary relocation from
Jonesville crosses a bridge, turns left and
follows the paved River Road 2. 0 miles east
to the Catamount Trail parking area. There
it turns into the woods and follows the blue
diamond blazed Catamount Trail 2.5 miles
south then southwest via several dirt roads and
woods trail. From dirt road, follow 2" x 6"
white plastic blazes 0.6 miles southwest along
old skid road to the Long Trail south of Wiley
Lodge.
   There is no parking along the River Road.
    Please park only in the designated parkin~
area in Jonesville or at the Catamount Trail
parking area. THANK YOU.
