Finally, several Bennington Section
members had special summer experiences.
For two and a half weeks in July, Linda
Tilgner, our president, hiked with a Sierra
Club group in the Austrian Tyrol. When
Linda returned from the meadows of Austria
to hiking in the Green Mountain woods, she
remarked, "I was again reminded of the nur-
turing and restorative power of our friendly
old mountains, forested and softened by time.
How lucky we are to live among them."
   The lure of the Canadian Rockies took
Barb and Charlie Hine on a hike led by leaders
of the 1985 GMC trip, Paul and Katie
Gilbert. After stopping to hike the North
Cascades and visit Carbin Glacier on Mt.
Ranier, they took the Vancouver train to
Banff where they ran into snow. This was
quite a shock after leaving a mid-August heat
wave. They hiked the high passes with snow
lasting a few days. Lots of wild life was
spotted: deer, moose, elk, sheep and the thrill
of watching a golden eagle up close. They en-
joyed miles of snow-covered peaks and
glaciers, but the most spectacular was the
Colombia Ice Field which flows into the
Pacific, Artie and eventually the Atlantic.

                                                                                      End to End Available
                                                                                    In November, the GMC will release a new
                                                                                 publication, End to End, Topographic Maps
                                                                                 of Vermont's Long Trail. This publication is
                                                                                 a set of 21 topographic maps that cover the
                                                                                 entire Long Trail. The maps use four-color
                                                                                 USGS quads for a base, and add Long Trail
                                                                                 information in red.
                                                                                    The maps were originally published by
                                                                                 Northern Cartographic of Burlington, in a
                                                                                 different format. Last winter the GMC pur-
                                                                                 chased the copyright and negatives from
                                                                                 Northern Cartographic, and updated the
                                                                                 maps with current trail information.
                                                                                    Updated trail information was gathered by
                                                                                 GMC Field Supervisor Ray Auger, and
                                                                                 former Northern Field Assistant Ben Davis.
                                                                                 Corrections were made to the original maps
                                                                                 by Middlebury College cartography students
