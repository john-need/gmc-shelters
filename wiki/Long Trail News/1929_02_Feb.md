                ously working on a construction program by which
                the Trail and shelters would be permanently loca-
                ted and completely constructed by January, 1931,
                in which year we hope to appropriately celebrate
            /   the twenty-first anniversary of the founding of the
                Club.
        /
                      The past year witnessed the completion of
    /           the Trail and the 44th camp. To provide adequate
                shelter for the entire Trail ten more camps are
                needed. We will exert every possible effort to
                have them finished by January, 1931.
                       As each camp costs about $250 a total ot
                $2250 must be raised. We earnestly hope you will
                feel that the benefits to be derived from the Long
                Trail warrant your joining in our final effort to
                realize an ambition of many years. Any contri·
I               bution will be most gratefully and appreciatively
                received. For a gift of $250 we would like the
