              PIONEER VALLEY SECTION
   Pioneer Valley members have cut out and blue blazed
the path to a new overspill camp site at Condgon camp.
It is located on a knoll about 150 yards above the outhouse.
The new site is expected to take some of the load off
Condgon camp, for those who have tents.
   Regular work parties in May cleared the trails of winter
debris. Extra help in this work was offered us by Dave
Ledger who took several members of his Christian Youth
Group, who cleared trail from Harmon Hill to Condgon.
   A variety of hikes have kept those members who par-
ticipated, quite busy during April and May. A couple of
weekend campouts were also offered, and quite a few
members attended the Annual Meeting in East Poultney,
over the Memorial weekend.
   We welcome one and all on our hikes, and information
on them may be obtained from our new Activities Chair-
man, Mason Ingram, who can be reached at his home ad-
dress of 15 Helen Circle, East Longmeadow, Mass.,
Telephone (413) 525-7177.
