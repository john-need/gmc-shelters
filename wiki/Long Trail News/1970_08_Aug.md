   If there were those present whose breakfast is "just a
cup of coffee," they were lost in the crowd of those happily
devouring a choice of pancakes, french toast, bacon or
smoky sausages, eggs to individual taste, and various other
tempting offerings. It is suspected that a few succumbed
to the temptation to go from breakfast to coffee break at
one sitting!
