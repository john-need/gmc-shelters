                                       THIRTY YEARS OLD !
   It was just thirty years ago to-day (March eleventh) that an enthusiastic
little group of Vermonters gathered at the VanN ess House in Burlington to
org:mize the Green Mountain Club which, to quote the Burlington Free
Press of the following day, "will undertake to promote interest in mountain
climbing in this state." James P. Taylor now the secretary of the State Chamber
of Commerce was the chief exponent of the idea and was elected the Club's first
presid!'lnt.
   Taylor and his friends probably had little idea of the results their organi-
zation would accomplish within the space of a comparatively few years or
of the importance the Long Trail would be to the State. They had faith, enthus-
iasm and a hundred dollars given the Club by M. E. Wheeler of Rutland
which they promptly put to work.
   From this small beginning has grown the present Green Mountain Club with
its Long Trail of 260 miles connecting Massachusetts and Canada, its many
miles of side trails and the many camps and shelters that together make one of
the best hiking trails in the country. There is also the Long Trail Lodge
Development at Sherburne Pass, the permanent patrol system, the guide
book and folders as well as a competent secretary to take care of all inquiries and
requests for information. Mr. Taylor and the others of the original group who - are
still living may well look at the present club with considerable satisfaction.
   The founding members gathered that day at Burlington were:-D. T. Page of
Bakersfield; 0. K. Hollister of Barre; Arthur W. Eddy of Bristol; Edward K.
Allen, C. W. Brownell, M. D. Chittenden, Clarence P. Cowles, Judge Seneca Hasel-
ton, J. L. Southwick and J. E. Tracy of Burlington; C. W. Gates of Franklin;
Elwin L. Ingalls of Hartford; Mason Stone of Montpelier; K. R. B. Flint of
Northfield; C. H. Morrill of Randolph; M. E. Wheeler and Rev. W. H. Spence
of Rutland; James P. Taylor of Saxtons River; H. G. Thomas of Stowe; C. H.
Drown of Wallingford; H. C. Whitehill of Waterbury and Frank H. Clark of Windsor.
