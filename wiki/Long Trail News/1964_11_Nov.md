              HAVE YOU HEARD?
   The Ski Touring Council is hard at work locating
and developing "white trails" which will be suitable
for cross country skiing.
   Interest in the sport of skiing has increased
tremendously in recent years and although new ski
areas are constantly being developed to meet this
need, many skiers are weary of the crowded slopes,
the long waits on lift lines and the up and down hill
routine.
   So if you are a skier who would enjoy the peace
and beauty of the woods in winter, try ski touring.
