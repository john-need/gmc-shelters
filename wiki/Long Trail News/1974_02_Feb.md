                   PRESIDENT'S MESSAGE
   A previous message mentioned an objective of en-
couragement of strong leadership for the future. A con-
scientious and knowledgable Nominations Committee,
consisting of Chairman Dick Metcalf, Pioneer, Betty
Weeks, Killington and Jack Harrington, Burlington has
been considering the key nominations to be made at the
May 25 Annual Meeting in North Bennington. They will
welcome your suggestions and observations.
   We welcome the new Green Mountain Club, Inc. Jay
Section at Newport, Vermont. 81 Charter members strong,
this hustling bunch of trail enthusiasts will take over the
northernmost segment of the Long Trail and shelters.
President Charles Monty is congratulated on a fine job
of organization. His picture will appear elsewhere in this
issue so that you will recognize him at the Annual.
   A heavy trail maintenance program is planned this year
by Chairman George Pearlstein, aided by leadership de-
veloped last season. The Caretakers will be out in force
and we are considering proposals for expanding our role
on both State and Federal land along the trail.
   Recent recreation visitors to Vermont have been nu-
merous but have come from less distant locations for the
most part. This may turn out to be the pattern next sum-
mer. We plan to be ready.
                           JOHN W. NUFFORT, Pres.
