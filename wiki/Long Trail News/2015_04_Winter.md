Sandy Stare: Builder of the Original Lost Pond Shelter

             olunteer Louis “Sandy”
             J. Stare Jr. of Craftsbury
             received special recognition
             for building the original Lost
Pond Shelter on the Long Trail at the
annual volunteer appreciation picnic in
September. The club recognized Sandy
fifty years later because his determina-
tion, ingenuity, and passion for the Long
Trail represent the characteristics of so
many GMC volunteers.
    In 1963 Sandy saw the new U.S Forest
Service shelters at Little Rock Pond, and
was inspired to build one. Using a Forest
Service blueprint, he constructed the
shelter at his Cape Cod home in winter
of 1965. He had to modify the design so
it could be disassembled, transported to
Vermont, and reassembled in the woods.
