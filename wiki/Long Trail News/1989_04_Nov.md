Veterans Affairs Secretary Edward Derwinski
presented each award recipient with an
engraved glass plaque during the ceremony.
An evening reception, followed the next day
by networking seminar and a special
ceremony for agricultural winners at the
Department of Agriculture, wrapped up the
festivities.
   The Green Mountain Club was one of
only six constituent organizations to win a
1988 award. The Club's Caretaker program
was specifically cited as an example of
successful volunteerism. This program posts
caretakers and their volunteer assistants at
heavily used sites on the Long Trail to educate
hikers, maintain trails and protect fragile
backcountry resources. (See 'Confessions of
an Addicted Volunteer Assistant Caretaker'
in the May, 1988 issue of The Long Trail News.)
   The Club was nominated for the award by
the Green Mountain National Forest. A state
"Take Pride in America" award earlier this
year earned the Club a place as a finalist in
the national competition.

administers the Take Pride in America
