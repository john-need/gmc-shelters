ANNUAL RANGE HIKE
   The Appalachian Mountain Club will sponsor the An-
nual Range Hike in the White Mountains of New Hamp-
sl1ire from Sat. July 15 thru Sun. July 23, 1972. Early
registration is desirable since each of three sections will ac-
commodate only 30 hikers (15 male, 15 female). For ex-
perienced bikers only, Section Able will traverse the Presi-
dential Range starting from Franconia Notch going to
Pinkham Notch and Section Bravo will traverse the same
route in reverse; while Section Charlie, based at Pinkham
Notch Camp, will take day trips less demanding from a
physical standpoint. The cost for each section will be $135
for members, GMC included. Further infor mation may be
obtained from Range Hike Co-ordinator: Cecil M. Jones
72 Chester St., Worcester, Mass. 01605. Telephone 617-
799-7212.

             Saturday, March 25, 1972
       Sage Hall, Smith College, Northampton, Mass.
  The 55th annual meeting of the New England Trail
Conference will focus this year on the theme "Trails on
Trial." All persons interested in hiking, trail maintenance,
conservation, and the out-of-doors are welcome for all or
any part of the day-long meeting. Advance registration by
Monday, March 20, is requested. There is a fee of $1.50
to cover conference costs, and a dinner charge of $3.75.

Conference.

registered at the 1971 conference. Additional forms are
available from the Secretary, Kathryn E. Burnett, 629
Florence Road, Northampton, Mass. 01060; please include
a stamped, self-addressed envelope.
