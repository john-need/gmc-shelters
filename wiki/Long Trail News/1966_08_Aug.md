                                    "WILDLANDS PROFILE STRIP"
                  At the Governor's Conference on Natural Beauty, May25-26, in Montpelier, a
               "Wildlands Profile Strip" along the ridges of the Green Mountains was proposed.
               GMC Trustees have endorsed the concept of this plan to preserve the scenic beauty
               and wilderness character of the Green Mts. the length of the State. Many indi-
               viduals and several other groups have also endorsed this plan. Space and time do
               not allow for details as we go to press, but we feel sure that members will be hear-
               ing and reading more about it soon.
