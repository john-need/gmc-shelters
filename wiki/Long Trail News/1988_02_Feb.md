              $150,000 APPROPRIATED TO
             NATIONAL PARK SERVICE FOR
            LONG TRAIL PROTECTION PLAN
         Senator Patrick Leahy was successful in his effort to make $150, 000 available in fiscal year 1988
      National Park Service appropriations for the purpose of developing a comprehensive protection
      plan for the Long Trail. The Senator has written to National Park Service Director William Mott
      to set up a meeting between Park Service officials and representatives of the Green Mountain
      Club. The objective of the meeting will be to develop a cooperative agreement for carrying out
      the necessary planning work. This would include the preparation of detailed tract maps and con-
      tracting for a baseline survey of the Long Trail route identified for protection.
         This is an exciting and important step forward in the Club's Long Trail protection effort. We
      extend our thanks and appreciation to Senator Leahy for his efforts.
                                                                                     Harry T. Peet, Jr.
                                                                                     Executive Director

Board of Directors meeting in Putney Vt. by
Joseph Cook, GMC President.
   The Senator was cited for his dedication to
protecting the country's air, water and land.

Public Works Committee during the last three
sessions of Congress, he led the fight for some
of the most important environmental legisla-
tion in the past two decades, including the
passage of bills to expand the Superfund pro-
gram to clean up toxic waste, to remove
asbestos from our schools, and to ensure the
safety of our drinking water.

tered and framed and included an attractive,
bright green representation of the Club's
letterhead.

Senator Robert T. Gannett, a long time friend
of the Club and Mrs. Aldie Gannett, one of
the honorary Co-Chairs of the Long Trail
Protection Fund, were in attendance.
   Two Brattleboro radio stations and a local
newspaper, the Brattleboro Reformer were there
to record the event and to interview Sen. Staf-
ford. The Senator accepted the Citation
