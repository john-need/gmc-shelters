           GMCAcquires Land for Lamoille
              Valley Trail Relocation
   A li~e over a y;ar'ago, follow.ing several years of scouting various trail. route alrerna-
  .l'\. aves, GMC s Board of Directors voted to relocate the Long Trail through the
   Lamoille River Valley. In a significant first step towards this relocation, the Green
   Mountain Club recently acquired sixty-five acres on Dry Ridge in Johnson from Ed-
   ward Buttolph. Currently, the Trail through the Lamoille Valley follows roads for five
   miles. The planned relocation will eliminate the road walk, greatly improving the
   safety and scenic quality of the trail experience.
      Before the relocation can take place, several additional tracts of land must be
   acquired and a footbridge over the Lamoille River must be constructed for the Long
   Trail. GMC has obtained contracts from two other landowners and is in the process of
   ~cquiring their four parcels. The acquisition of the Buttolph Tract involved an exchange
   of GMC-owned land in Eden not near the Trail. GMC: appreciates the Buttolphs'
   support of the Long Trail protection program; in 1992 Ed and his wife Barbara sold
   land in Bolton to the Club for the Trail.
