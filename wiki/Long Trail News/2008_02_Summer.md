    First, get off high ground. If you can
hear thunder, you are within range of
lightning strikes. Always retreat from
high ground unless going uphill is the
quickest route to safety (such as when
near a shelter). If you can't make a
quick exit, find shelter in the trees.
Small, densely-packed ridgeline trees
are better options than a tall tree in a
field. Next make sure you're not stand-
ing in water-ground strikes can find
you. Remove anything that can con-
duct electricity, like your pack frame.
Crouch to reduce your profile. Put on
rain gear and some warm clothing like
a wool hat-one never knows how
long a storm will last. Be prepared for
noise and wind. Make sure your gear
is secure. I once saw a pack cover blow
