BOLTON RELOCATION FINISHED!       On October 17, 1982 the Burlington Sec-
                                      tion officiaJly opened the reroute of the Long
                                      Trail between a point north of Duck Brook
                                      Shelter and Bolton Mountain. About 25
                                      Section members turned out for a series of
                                      hikes and a cookout, which were held despite
                                      the season's first snowfall.
                                          The Section decided to reroute the Trail
                                      when advised by one of the landowners over
                                      which a greater part of the LT in chat area
                                      passes that logging <1!Jd other development in
                                      the Bolton Valley area might detract from
                                      th wilderness aspects of the stretch of trail
                                      affected. It was decided to move the rail
                                      higher up on the ridgeline to avoid the po-
                                      tential development and to take advantage of
                                      some fine views afforded by the higher ele-
                                      vations.
                                          With several years notice of the potential
                                      changes, the GMC had plenty of time to seek
                                      a n w route and accomplish the surveying,
                                      cutting and marking necessary to establish
                                      the new passage. Section work parries coiled
                                      steadily. and this summer the Long Trail
                                      Patrol worked on the reroute and helped
                                      bring it to completion.
                                          With all the effort involved, a formal
                                      opening ceremony was deemed proper, and
                                      was scheduled for Sunday, October 17th.
                                      Unfortunately, the day started with pouring
                                      rain which turned to snow at the higher ele-
                                      vations of the new route . The rain/snow
                                      combined with the thick coating of fallen
                                      leaves on the ground made the hikers who
                                      rum d out slip back two feet for every three
                                      walk di On top of that, the h avy clouds pre-
                                      cluded any enjoyment of the new section's
                                      excellent lookouts.
                                          Once gathered at noon at Duck Brook
                                      Shelter, the process of starting a fire to warm
                                      cold bodies and to heat up various lunches
                                      was the main order of business. With every-
                                       thing combustible being soaking wet, a lot of
                                      very close attention had to be paid to the tiny
                                      fire , but th persistence of Burlington Sec-
                                       tion members paid off and a respectable con-
                                      flagration was ultimately achieved.
                                          After lunch, a ribbon cutting ceremony
                                       was held just north of the Duck Brook
                                      Shelter. Burlington Section President Andy
                                      Squires read out a long list of all those who
                                       had worked so hard to complete the reroute.
                                       One of the finest lookouts on the new Trail
                                      was named after Burlington Section member
                                      Jack Harrington, in recognition of his special
                                      efforts on many a weekend. A wooden
                                       plaque signifying "Harrington's View" was
                                       unveiled, and will be put up at the site of the
                                       overlook.
                                          The relocated LT, like the old route, is
                                       mostly over lands of the Bolton Valley Cor-
                                       poration, which operates the ski area of the
                                      same name. Ralph R. DesLauriers, Presi-
                                       dent of Bolton Valley, was the first land-
                                       owner in the state to sign a trail protection
                                       agreement when this program started back
                                       in 1973.
                                          Guidebook updates will be published as
                                       soon as available.
