                                                         Now we have Coast-to-Coasting! End-to-Ending the Long Trail's 262 miles is a notable
                                                      achievement but what two New Hampshire men accomplished this past summer makes
                                                      that look like a walk in the backyard. On July 24 at Sant Monica, Calif., Scott King and
                                                      George Hormell completed a 3,281-mile stroll which they had begun on March 15 at
                                                      Portland, Me. The two were accompanied by another man who drove a van filled with
                                                      food and supplies (at night the three slept in the van). King's and Hormell's only complaint
                                                      at the end of the trip was, what else, sore feet!

                                                      Club, her tremendous hard work in the past 22 years which benefited all GMC members,
