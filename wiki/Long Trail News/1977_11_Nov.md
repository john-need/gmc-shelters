           In June this year Thomas W . Luce of Osoyoos, British
        Columbia in Canada presented the Club with a two-volume,
        248-page typewritten manuscript of his work, "Saga Of The
        Long Trail." The poem, which describes Luce's End-to-Ending
        of the LT over a period of six years, itself involved ten years

        days in Vermont.

        in 1972 after long service in various operational and admini-
        strative positions with the Canadian Immigration Department.
