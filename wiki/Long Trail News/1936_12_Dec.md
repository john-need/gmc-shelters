A WARNING

The experience of Dr. L. E. Judd of Brandon should prove a warning to hikers not to travel the Long Trail (or any other trail) alone. An accident might happen which would disable the solitary hiker and render him helpless and perhaps endanger limb or life unless assistance should arrive quickly.
