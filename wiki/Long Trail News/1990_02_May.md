  Camp Thorpe is within four miles of the Long Trail at Brandon Gap. It is an ideal site to spend a week enjoying the Vermont
outdoors. There will be hiking and other activities during the day, evening programs, cooperative babysitting and lots of fun.
Come for a day, an evening, a weekend or all week but don't miss out! The square dance should be a lot of fun .

  You do not have to stay at Camp Thorpe to take part in Intersectional activities. If you live nearby or prefer to stay in a
motel or bed and breakfast we would love to have you participate. For last minute changes in schedules or to register to take
part in an activity please call 802-247-9563. This is the pay phone at the recreation hall. The best time to reach leaders will
be from 7:30 pm to 10:00 pm.
