         DISESTABLISHMENT
                OF
         MONTREAL SECTION

      The Montreal Section of the Club has
   been inactive for several years now,
   despite several efforts to rekindle
   enough interest to maintain an active
   organization. The official disestablish-
   ment of the Montreal Section will be on
   the agenda of the May 28, 1988 Board
   of Directors Meeting. Those few re-
   maining individual members of the Sec-
   tion have been contacted in the hope
   they wish to remain GMC members.
